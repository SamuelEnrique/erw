B1. Derived `grid_network_nodes` (one row per BA: name, which ISO or none, latest
    demand where held, latest carbon intensity where held, total interchange volume)

B2. The page does not read Supabase for hourly links. The build writes a static
    snapshot, site/data/grid_network.json (nodes, fixed 3D positions, 168 hours of
    links), regenerated by the daily run after the connector. Only the small nodes
    table goes into Supabase if the live set stays under 345 MB after VACUUM FULL;
    otherwise it goes into the JSON too.
B3. Positions: compute the force layout once with a fixed seed, store x, y, z per
    node in the snapshot, never re-settle on the page, so a student's mental map holds.
B4. Page /network (nav under Grid, first): 3d-force-graph, drag to rotate, scroll to
    zoom, auto-rotate slowly until touched. Sphere size = demand (held BAs) or
    interchange volume (others, marked so); color = carbon intensity on a green to
    cardinal scale, grey where not held; link width = MW; directional particles along
    each link in the direction of flow, speed scaled to MW. A time slider over the 168
    hours with play and pause, the hour shown in UTC and Eastern. Click a node: a card
    with its name, demand, intensity and top three flows; the seven ISOs link to
    /grid/<iso>. A one-paragraph cited explainer: three interconnections, ERCOT's thin
    ties, what a balancing authority is (EIA). A fallback message where WebGL is not
    available. Stanford palette. Each /grid/<iso> page links to /network.
