**dm_live_set, FAILED tables: ['sources'].** On 15 to 18 October: "MISMATCH sources: CSV 315, Supabase 316", the extra row OPEC's publications, loaded by a run on lab-desktop on 15 October (15:32 UTC) whose tables were never committed; session 305 (`4b2fb212`, `1ca1684e`) put the row back in `sources.csv` and committed those tables. That form is session 374's of 20 October (the rows landed on main from the branches or from Supabase's own table).

**dm_commit (git pull --rebase exit 128: committed here, not pushed).** The run of 15 October on lab-desktop left eight tracked news and deal tables uncommitted ("You have unstaged changes"), so each later pull refused. Fixed by session 305 (`1ca1684e`): `run_data_machine.sh` sets such files aside in a named stash before the pull, and `4b2fb212` committed the tables.

**Redivis "Cannot export more than 100GB within a 30 day period" (the daily run's restore, the Roundup's restore, dm_zone_redivis's erw_headers).** `run_daily.sh`'s first step (`upload.py --restore`) exported about 2.5 GB from Redivis every run (48 rolling-window tables; `eia930_all_emissions` alone 1.1 GB); with the reruns, the Roundup and `sync.py` that passed 100 GB in 30 days on 15 October, and the daily run stopped at its first step each day (the Roundup of week 42 was not sent). Fixed by session 305 (`1ca1684e`, on main at 06:02 UTC on 19 October): `warehouse/restore_tables.py` restores from GitHub's cache first, then the archive bucket, Redivis last under 2 GB a day; `roundup.yml` and `sync.py` follow the same order. The Roundup's failure of 19 October 00:37 UTC came before the landing. No export failure since; a new limit is reached instead (Redivis's 10 GB of free storage, session 363).
