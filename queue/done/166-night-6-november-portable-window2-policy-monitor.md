## Why this chain (the owner's words)

The policy and regulatory monitor (tool 12) is the platform's watch on market-moving policy: FERC orders, NRC decisions, permitting, tax credits and their guidance, tariffs, interconnection rules, LNG export approvals, leasing, state actions, each with a plain-language read of what it moves. The warehouse already scores policy stories daily (`policy_score`: 1,959 of 1,991 scored on 30 October, 388 at significance 5 or more) and holds `policy_reads` and `policy_actions`, and the policy pages landed on 5 November. What it lacks is the tool: one place where a reader filters the actions by body, instrument, sector and grid, sees each action's date, status and next step, reads what it changes in one sentence with the figure it touches, and follows the trail to the document. This chain builds that from the tables the warehouse holds, with nothing invented and no point of view: the monitor says what an order does and which table it moves; the opinion is the owner's, elsewhere.
