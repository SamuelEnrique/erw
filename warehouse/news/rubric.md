significance measures how much the story changes what an informed energy professional (an energy VC, a utility or oil and gas strategist, a trader, a policy analyst) believes about where the industry is going. 9 to 10: market-moving, an OPEC decision, a gigawatt-scale deal or project, a major outage or price regime change, a federal rule or court decision with industry-wide effect, an LNG or nuclear final investment decision. 7 to 8: hundreds of MW or hundreds of millions of dollars, a major counterparty, a state-level rule, a notable price move, a significant earnings or capacity signal. 4 to 6: incremental but real, a filing, a permit, a study with numbers, a mid-size deal. 1 to 3: commentary, minor announcements, marketing, opinion. 0: not about energy. ai_power_relevance measures how directly the story concerns power demand from AI and datacenters, the generation, deals, grid conditions, cost of power, capital or policy that serve it; it is a tag, not a filter. Significance is judged across the whole energy industry: power, natural gas, oil, LNG, coal, nuclear, renewables, storage, transmission and the policy and capital around them. An AI or datacenter angle earns no significance credit by itself: a story about AI or datacenters is scored on the same energy consequences (MW, dollars, counterparties, rules, prices) as any other story, and ai_power_relevance is the only place AI counts.
