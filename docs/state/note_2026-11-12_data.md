## Read these first

- **Five new public tables,** written, validated, archived, in coverage, in the release package and the Stanford draft, held from the live set, each with a method note:
  - `usgs_comcat_earthquakes_south_central`: the 16,000 earthquakes of magnitude 2.5 and above in the bounding box of Oklahoma, Kansas, Texas and New Mexico since 2010. Oklahoma's of magnitude 3 and up peak at 885 in 2015 and fall from 2016 while its oil production kept rising to 2019: the injection limits, not the oil.
  - `epa_tri_energy_releases_annual`: EPA's Toxics Release Inventory for the electric utilities, refineries, gas processing plants, coal mines and bulk terminals, 2015 to 2023 (128,161 forms). The utilities' releases fell from 432.6 to 194.6 million lb and their mercury from 102,399 to 38,313 lb. A new unit, `g`, for the dioxins (Decision 66).
  - `eurostat_renewable_capacity_annual`: Eurostat's renewable and waste capacity, 39 European countries, 1990 to 2024; the countries Eurostat's terms allow for commercial reuse only (the UK and Kosovo left out). Wind equals IRENA's to the MW in five large countries.
  - `owid_ember_electricity_annual`: Ember's electricity demand and power sector emissions for 239 countries and regions, through Our World in Data's catalog; the only two columns of its energy dataset whose every source has a verdict. U.S. demand is within 0.2% of EIA's every year.
  - `epa_lmop_landfill_projects`: EPA's Landfill Methane Outreach Program, 2,641 landfills and 542 operational landfill gas projects (EPA's own counts, checked on every run), 1,745 MW.
- **Not taken, said so:** the IRS's energy credits and IRENA's capacity (chain 068 took them first); FEMA's National Risk Index (its host resets the connection); the USGS's thermoelectric water use (ScienceBase's robots file disallows every reader by default); Berkeley Lab's retail price and cost data (on emp.lbl.gov only, which refuses the reader).
