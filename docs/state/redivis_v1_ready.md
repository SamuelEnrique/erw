# Redivis version 1: what is ready, and the one click (session 2882, 5 November 2026; brought up to date by session 2929, 6 November 2026)

1. **The draft:** Stanford's `datapages` dataset `b9wm` (https://stanford.redivis.com/datasets/b9wm-3hb080mtm). It holds the tables whose ERW verdict in `docs/licenses.md` is public (467 reconciled on 5 November at about 22:30 UTC: 393 match their CSV, 74 differ by the days since their last upload, 0 missing) and the three metadata tables. Chain 147 added `subregion_demand_growth` (3,918 rows, counted equal) and refreshed `erw_headers` (5,585 lines). Chain 157 (session 2959, 6 November about 08:50 UTC) uploaded its four derived tables, each counted equal: `generator_pipeline_by_grid` (5,147 rows), `iso_queue_snapshot_summary` (570), `nuclear_fleet_status` (15,048), `gas_balance_weekly` (40,507), and refreshed `erw_headers` (5,628 lines); they are held from the v1 package (prepared before them) until you add them. Chain 153 (session 2929, 6 November about 06:17 UTC) uploaded `ercot_transmission_projects` (ERCOT's October TPIT, 2,203 rows) and `ercot_node_transmission` (569 rows), each counted equal, and refreshed `erw_headers` (5,585 lines); reconciled after: 469 tables, 385 match, 83 differ by the days since their last upload, 0 missing.
2. **The package:** `docs/redivis/release-v1/` (README, `tables.csv`, `variables.csv`) describes 136 tables (10 entities, 10 events, 116 series), each with its license and its source. `release_v1.py --check` passes against the license record.
3. **Not in it:** every internal or unsettled table, `utility_large_load_tariffs`, `large_load_connection_rules` (session 2924, unsettled as the tariffs are) and `ercot_wind_solar_hsl_monthly` held as not on Redivis (`release_v1.STORAGE_HELD`; the first unsettled: the IURC's statewide terms are on record as "needs written permission", and the Georgia and Oregon commissions have no verdict). Nothing has been released.
4. **What you click:** on the dataset's page, "Version 1 (draft)", read the diff, then "Release". Before you click, re-run `python warehouse/redivis/upload.py --reconcile --target stanford` if you want the 83 differing tables (6 November) brought to today's rows (`upload.py --target stanford --changed`, under the data lock, never in the background).
5. **After the release:** the next uploads write a version 2 draft. Nothing scheduled releases.
