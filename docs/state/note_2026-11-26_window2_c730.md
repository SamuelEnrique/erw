# Note of chain 730 (lab-desktop second window, 26 November 2026): the ERW forecast

Sessions 10600 and 10601. Written at 12:50 UTC and kept current until the chain stops. Model spend USD 0; no warehouse table written; no data lock taken.

## What was built

- **Demand, 29 places:** the 13 EIA regions and the 16 balancing authorities of at least 5,000 MW. Every place uses the regression: one least-squares fit an hour, refit every day on the 730 days before it, with no weather (no dated weather forecast is held).
- **Day-ahead price, 6 places on 3 hubs:** TEX and ERCO on ERCOT HB_NORTH use yesterday's price. CENT and SWPP on SPP North and CAL and CISO on CAISO SP15 use the regression.
- **The peak, and two to seven days ahead.** Every horizon beats "the same hour a week ago" (145 of 145).
- **A separate line "with the weather known"** (the regression given the observed temperature; not a forecast, never the headline): it would beat the operator in 12 places instead of 8. In TEX, NE and BPAT the weather is less than half of the gap.
- **Walk-forward backtest** from 1 July 2019 to 24 November 2026. Nothing is read that was published after the hour the forecast is taken as made: 20:00 UTC the day before for demand, 14:00 UTC for price. Tests hold the rule.
- **Live ledgers:** the first real forecasts were made at 11:00 UTC on 26 November, for 27 November: 696 demand rows and 144 price rows. They are never rewritten.
