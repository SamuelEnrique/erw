  - `ons_brazil_balance_hourly`: Brazil's generation by source, load and interchange an hour by subsystem from ONS (CC BY), 2020 to 22 October 2026 (1,790,640 rows). Brasília time is inferred from the solar peak.
  - `eia_crude_quality_monthly`: the API gravity and sulfur of the crude U.S. refineries run, 1985 to July 2026. It needed a new unit, `degAPI` (Decision 73).

  - `eia_shale_gas_reserves_annual`: shale gas proved reserves and their changes by state and district, 2007 to 2021. EIA's route ends there, so this is a closed record.
