  - `eia_aeo_emm_regions`: AEO2026's Table 54 for the 25 EMM regions, capacity, additions, retirements, generation, sales and prices to 2050, the Counterfactual Baseline case.
  - `lbnl_ppa_prices`: Berkeley Lab's 1,133 wind and solar PPA prices, one row a contract, CC BY 4.0.
  - `ercot_node_transmission` (derived): ERCOT's TPIT projects by the county of 569 placed resource nodes. Fills the siting gaps' "transmission projects by county" for those nodes.
