  - `census_cbp_hosting_annual` (1,836): County Business Patterns for NAICS 518210 (data processing and hosting) by state, 2012 to 2023. It counts the industry's workforce, not its power: Virginia's commercial sales rose 60 percent while its hosting establishments stayed near 600.
