  - `eia_net_imports_monthly` (public): EIA's U.S. net imports of crude oil and petroleum products by partner, January 2010 to July 2026. The U.S. is a net exporter in 75 of the 199 months, every month from October 2021.
  - `eurostat_electricity_production_annual` (public): Eurostat's gross electricity production by fuel for 39 areas, 1990 to 2024 (58 products). TOTAL equals its top groups plus pumped hydro and batteries in every country year.
  - `statcan_generating_capacity_annual` (public): Statistics Canada's installed capacity by type and class of producer, by province, 2006 to 2025 (no figure for 2012).
