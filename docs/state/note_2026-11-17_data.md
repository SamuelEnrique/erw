## Read these first

- **Seven new tables,** written, validated, archived, in coverage and held from the live set, each with a method note. The six public ones are also in the release package and the Stanford draft.
  - `statcan_electricity_generation_monthly`: Canada's electricity generation by province, class of producer and type, a month, 2008 to July 2026 (Statistics Canada 25-10-0015-01, 58,361 rows).
  - `bsee_offshore_incidents`: 4,818 incidents on the Outer Continental Shelf, 2018 and 2020 to 2024, with fires, injuries, fatalities, loss of well control and BSEE's summaries. Incidents per 100 million barrels of Gulf oil roughly doubled (78 to 159).
  - `nrc_rop_findings`: 18,240 NRC inspection findings and violations at 66 reactor sites, 1999 to May 2025, each with its cornerstone and significance color (15 red, at five sites). The NRC's file stops at 12 May 2025.
  - `statcan_oil_gas_supply_monthly`: Canada's crude oil and natural gas supply and disposition by province, a month, 2016 to 2026, in cubic metres. A new unit, `m3` (Decision 70). Canada's crude exports to the U.S. are 95 to 98 percent of EIA's imports from Canada, and its gas exports 98 to 101 percent.
  - `ntd_transit_energy_annual`: the fuel and electricity each U.S. transit agency used, by mode, 2022 to 2025 (the FTA's National Transit Database). Electric propulsion grew from 5.5 to 6.7 TWh, and battery charging from 49 to 112 GWh.
  - `epa_needs_units`: EPA's NEEDS for the 2025 reference case, 38,030 units in its four sheets (active, retired by 2028, hardwired, dropped). Active and hardwired capacity is 97 percent of EIA-860M's operating fleet in the same states.
  - `usaspending_doe_obligations` (internal): DOE's budget, obligations and award obligations by category, fiscal 2017 to 2026, from USAspending.gov. DOE's grants rose from about 3 billion USD a year to 28.8 billion in fiscal 2025, and stand at 2.6 billion in fiscal 2026 to August.
