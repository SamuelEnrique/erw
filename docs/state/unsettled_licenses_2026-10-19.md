# The 19 tables whose license is not settled

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), session 324, 19 October 2026. For Samuel. Nothing was sent to anyone. A table is "not settled" when a publisher on it was not read, or its page states no terms of reuse (`docs/licenses.md`, "Not settled: a publisher was not read"; session 311). One line a publisher: the page read (the record's number in `warehouse/metadata/license_reads.csv`), its tables, and the sentence a permission email would need. The sentences are drafts for you to send or not; the address is the one the record holds, or none.
