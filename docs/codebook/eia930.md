### `eia930_all_demand`

One balancing authority's (or the Lower 48's) hourly demand or day-ahead demand forecast in one hour, from EIA's API, the seven ISO authorities and US48.

Shape: series. Keys: `entity`, `variable`, `ts_utc`. License: public; tier: source. Rows read on 2026-11-20: 30528.

- **Time:** ts_utc is the hour's start, UTC (EIA's hourly period is the hour's end; one hour is subtracted).
- **Revisions:** Merged by key (entity, variable, ts_utc): the latest retrieval replaces earlier rows, older rows are kept. vintage is empty: EIA publishes no release time per value and revises EIA-930 values.
- **Source:** eia:electricity/rto/region-data Form EIA-930, Hourly Electric Grid Monitor: hourly demand, day-ahead demand forecast, net generation and interchange by balancing authority (https://www.eia.gov/electricity/gridmonitor/)
- **Method note:** [`docs/methods/eia930_all_demand.md`](../methods/eia930_all_demand.md)
- **Cite as:** U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA). Form EIA-930, Hourly Electric Grid Monitor: hourly demand, day-ahead demand forecast, net generation and interchange by balancing authority. Via the Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), table `eia930_all_demand`.
