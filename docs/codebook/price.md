# Codebook: price

Drawn from `warehouse/metadata/variables/` by `warehouse/codebook/build_docs.py`; never written by hand. The column names many tables share (`ts_utc`, `value`, `entity` and the others) are defined once in [`_shared.yaml`](../../warehouse/metadata/variables/_shared.yaml) and in the [index](README.md).

**What the family holds.** 4 public tables (4 series; tiers: 4 derived); 4 documented in full.

- [`price_board_latest`](#price_board_latest): One hub or zone's value of one price board measure for one market (day-ahead or real-time), over its latest days.
- [`price_board_peak_offpeak`](#price_board_peak_offpeak): One ISO main hub's peak, off-peak or all-hours price measure for one market, per complete operating day of the last 90, or per ERCOT operating year since 2015.
- [`price_board_spreads`](#price_board_spreads): One day's spark spread, implied heat rate or day-ahead mean at an ISO main hub, or that day's Henry Hub gas price or Brent less WTI oil spread.
- [`price_years_by_window`](#price_years_by_window): One economics page's figure (a battery's or generator's revenue, or a datacenter's cost) for one grid, price point, configuration and window of years held.
