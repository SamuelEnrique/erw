# Codebook: policy

Drawn from `warehouse/metadata/variables/` by `warehouse/codebook/build_docs.py`; never written by hand. The column names many tables share (`ts_utc`, `value`, `entity` and the others) are defined once in [`_shared.yaml`](../../warehouse/metadata/variables/_shared.yaml) and in the [index](README.md).

**What the family holds.** 3 public tables (3 events; tiers: 3 model_extracted); 3 documented in full.

- [`policy_action_tags`](#policy_action_tags): One tag (large_load, interconnection, transmission_cost or tax_credit) given to one policy action of policy_actions by a written rule applied by code (no model reads a row).
- [`policy_actions`](#policy_actions): One policy action (a Federal Register rule, proposed rule or notice of DOE, FERC, EPA, NRC, BLM, Interior, Treasury or the IRS on energy, or a news release of NRC, DOE, the PUCT or the CPUC), with fields read from its record and text and a model's score.
- [`policy_reads`](#policy_reads): One model's impact read of one policy action scored 5 or more (what changes, who is affected, the direction of the effect, the timeline, a plain read), each field kept only where its quotations stand word for word in the action's own text.

### `policy_actions`

One policy action (a Federal Register rule, proposed rule or notice of DOE, FERC, EPA, NRC, BLM, Interior, Treasury or the IRS on energy, or a news release of NRC, DOE, the PUCT or the CPUC), with fields read from its record and text and a model's score.

Shape: events. Keys: `event_id`. License: public; tier: model_extracted. Rows read on 2026-11-20: 2063.

- **Time:** event_date is the publication date (Register) or the release date (news), a date. text_read_at, scored_at and model_rechecked_at are UTC.
- **Revisions:** Every run reads the Register window (from 2025-10-01) again and merges news releases; a document's printed text is read once.

CPUC news; DOE newsroom (RSS); Federal Register documents API; NRC news releases (RSS); PUCT news releases. Via the Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), table `policy_actions`.
