# Codebook: utility

Drawn from `warehouse/metadata/variables/` by `warehouse/codebook/build_docs.py`; never written by hand. The column names many tables share (`ts_utc`, `value`, `entity` and the others) are defined once in [`_shared.yaml`](../../warehouse/metadata/variables/_shared.yaml) and in the [index](README.md).

**What the family holds.** 5 public tables (1 entities, 3 events, 1 series; tiers: 3 model_extracted, 2 source); 5 documented in full.

- [`utility_large_load_queue_totals`](#utility_large_load_queue_totals): One US utility's large-load queue figure as the utility or its regulator prints it (a dated total of requested or committed load, or a count of requests or customers), one row a publisher, from the ERW's research file of 13 November 2026.
- [`utility_large_load_rule_changes`](#utility_large_load_rule_changes): One filing, order, effective date, amendment, suspension, hearing or other change to a US utility's large-load rule, 15 August to 13 November 2026, as the ERW's research pass read it in the document.
- [`utility_large_load_rules`](#utility_large_load_rules): One US utility's (or one state commission's) rule for connecting large loads, such as data centers, with its terms as the documents print them, read by the ERW's research pass of 13 November 2026.
- [`utility_large_load_tariffs`](#utility_large_load_tariffs): One figure (a minimum load, a demand charge, an energy charge, a term) that one utility's filed large-load schedule prints, as of the schedule's effective date.
- [`utility_service_territories`](#utility_service_territories): One utility serving one county of one state or territory, as EIA's Form EIA-861 service territory sheet lists the pair for 2025.

### `utility_large_load_tariffs`

One figure (a minimum load, a demand charge, an energy charge, a term) that one utility's filed large-load schedule prints, as of the schedule's effective date.

Shape: series. Keys: `entity`, `variable`, `ts_utc`. License: public; tier: source. Rows read on 2026-11-20: 86.

- **Time:** ts_utc is the schedule's effective date at 00:00:00Z (a date, not an instant); freq is blank, a tariff in force from that day.
- **Revisions:** The filed rows are rewritten whole from the registry (warehouse/sources/large_load_tariffs/) by each run, keeping only other connectors' rows (OpenEI's, none written); schedules held for a license are named in the header and not written.
- **Source:** the registry warehouse/sources/large_load_tariffs/ (one file a schedule, each figure with its page and words), filled by reading each commission's filed copy. No request is made by this connector.
- **Method note:** [`docs/methods/large_load_tariffs.md`](../methods/large_load_tariffs.md)
- **Cite as:** the state commissions (filed tariffs), read into the ERW's registry. Large loads' filed tariffs (warehouse/sources/large_load_tariffs). Via the Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), table `utility_large_load_tariffs`.
