### `eia860_wind_generators`

One wind generator from Form EIA-860's wind schedule (3_2) for data year 2025, sheets Operable, Retired and Canceled, with its turbines, their maker, model, design wind speed, wind class and hub height.

Shape: entities. Keys: `entity_id`. License: public; tier: source. Rows read on 2026-11-20: 1745.

- **Time:** No time column; vintage is the data year (2025). status_date is the first day of the month of operation (operable) or of retirement (retired), empty for the canceled and the indefinitely postponed.
- **Revisions:** A snapshot; each run replaces the table whole with the newest final year EIA's page links, so an earlier year is not kept.
- **Source:** eia:eia860:wind U.S. Energy Information Administration, Form EIA-860 Annual Electric Generator Report, 3_2_Wind_Y2025.xlsx (https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860/)
- **Method note:** [`docs/methods/eia860_schedules.md`](../methods/eia860_schedules.md)
