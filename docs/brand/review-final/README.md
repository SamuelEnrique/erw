## How this folder was made

- `node runs/session242/final/shots.mjs <base-url> docs/brand/review-final [page ...]` (kept on the code machine, not in git): the internal view of a local build of this branch, each page at 1280 and at 390 px wide. With pages named it reshoots those only and replaces their lines of `facts.jsonl`; `node runs/session242/final/readme.mjs docs/brand/review-final` then writes this file again.
- A picture is `<address>_<width>.png` (every run of characters that are not letters or digits is a hyphen; `/` is `home`). **A picture shows a page's first 6,000 px at most** (40 of the 90 pages are taller at one width or both); the facts are of the whole page. **A picture is at half size** (640 px wide for 1280, 195 px wide for 390) and quantized to 16 colors, to keep 180 pictures under the folder's 12 MB (they are 11.1 MB; at full size they were 23.7 MB), so a map or a photograph looks posterized.
- `facts.jsonl`, one line a page: what the table is built from. A color is "outside the tokens" when the computed `fill`, `stroke`, `color` or `background-color` of an element that is drawn is none of the values the brand section of `site/app/tokens.css` declares (white, fog, rule, captions, ink, cardinal and its darker step, blue, light grey) and none of the subject palettes the file's first block keeps (fuels, durations, up and down, not held, not reported). `old:` marks a value the brand replaced (the captions grey, the rule and the panel of before). A tint of a token (the token with an opacity) counts as the token.
- **What the detector cannot see:** a chart or a map drawn on a canvas (the row says how many), a legend that is not named "legend" in its markup, a series that is not named on its chart, and anything a page draws only after a press (an answer on `/ask`, a run on `/thesis`, a submitted form, a folded block that is closed). Those rows carry what the earlier reports saw by eye, marked "earlier:" with the session.
- "Console exceptions" counts the two loads together. "On the brand": **yes** is the wrapper, nothing measured and nothing the detector cannot read; **partly** is the wrapper with something measured (a color, a font, a rounded corner, a shadow, a band, a legend, a wide table, a sideways scroll, the green of "down"), or a chart or map on a canvas, or a look an earlier report of the chain judged partly; **no** is no wrapper; **outside by ruling** is the home page and the Games pages.
