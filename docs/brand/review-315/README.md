# Session 315: the Research tool, its form, before and after

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), 19 October 2026. Shots of `/research` in the internal view at 1280 and 390 px: before
(production, session 314's landing) and after (this session's local build). Each shot is one section of the page (the
section's element), since the whole page runs to several screens.

What to look at:

- `ask_before_*.png` against `ask_after_*.png`: "Ask for an analysis". Before: the finding flow alone (step 1, step 2).
  After: the reader's own form first (the question with its count of 500, the tables to read chosen by name from the
  public tables of the live set, an email for the answer with the line that says what is done with it), then "Or
  choose a finding and its inputs" with the flow as it was. At 390 the table buttons wrap and the form fits the width.
- `today_after_*.png`: "Today's chart" with its sentence and, under it, "The record's figures" (the record's own
  numbers, each a checked number).
- `week_after_*.png`: "This week's chart" with its callouts, each figure a checked number.
- `library_before_*.png` against `library_after_*.png`: the findings library's first card. The callout figures are the
  same words; each is now a checked number (a data-check key naming the card and the figure's place in its JSON). A
  figure of NYISO or ISO-NE reads "license needed" from 21 October 2026 (the shots of 19 October show the figures).

Every number on the page is what it was (`check-values` on `/research`: the session's report).
