## Placing values in time, and what is filled

`ts_utc` is the year's 1 January at 00:00:00Z (Eurostat's TIME_PERIOD, a year), a label for the year, not an instant; the capacity is the year's as each country reports it. 35 years, 1990 to 2024. Every line of the answer for the areas kept has a value, so the 159,060 rows are the 160,114 lines less Kosovo's 1,054, left out under Eurostat's terms. Zeros are kept as Eurostat prints them, including those that stand for a kind or a flow a country does not split out (above). Nothing is filled, and no total is made here: EU27_2020 and EA21 are Eurostat's, and in every row the EU's figure is the sum of the members that have a row (Checks).

## Where the description and the data disagree

1. **Commissioning "by fuel group ... a year from 1990".** The docstring's first lines and header line 2 describe the capacity commissioned and decommissioned "by fuel group", for every area, a year from 1990. Both are given for 23 of the 27 codes (not TOTAL, hydro RA100, wind RA300 or solar photovoltaic RA420), and for 517 of 1,294 area-years. 16 countries give them only from 2020, 2021 or 2022. The EU's and the euro area's commissioning before then is the sum of the few members that report, from Denmark, Spain and Ireland alone in 1990.
2. **EU27_2020 and EA21 as "Eurostat's own totals".** The docstring's words hold (no sum is made here), but in 3,487 EU rows some members have no row, and there the EU's figure is the sum of the members that do. Germany's 2021 total, 60,036 MW short of its parts (its solar photovoltaic), carries into the EU's and euro area's 2021 totals: the EU's net capacity falls from 962.5 GW (2020) to 931.9 GW (2021) while the sum of its parts rises from 962.8 to 992.4 GW.
3. **The renewables comparison, "country-years".** `SESSION_2356_REPORT.md`: "Wind 1,244 of 1,294, Solar photovoltaic 1,155 of 1,269, Hydro 1,275 of 1,294" country-years. These counts include EU27_2020 and EA21 (70 area-years each for wind and hydro). The 38 countries alone give 1,200 of 1,224, 1,128 of 1,199 and 1,210 of 1,224.
4. **"Romania 2023, where Eurostat's two data sets disagree."** The report gives this as the reason for the largest gaps. `eurostat_renewable_capacity_annual` holds 0.0 for Romania's hydro, wind and solar photovoltaic in 2023, against 6,687.2, 3,026.8 and 2,988.0 MW here. 5. **"N9000 nuclear."** The docstring and the report's "nuclear 97 GW" read Eurostat's "Nuclear fuels and other fuels n.e.c.", which `node` gives in full. In 2024 it is above zero only in the 12 members with nuclear plants and in the aggregates, so the value reads as nuclear. The "other fuels n.e.c." are X9900, a separate code.
