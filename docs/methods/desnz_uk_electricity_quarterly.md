## Placing values in time, and what is filled

`ts_utc` is the quarter's first day at 00:00:00Z (the column header "Quarter <n> <year>"). "[x]" (no figure) has no row; nothing is filled. Totals are DESNZ's rows, never sums made here; a sum over the fuels must leave them out, and total_wind with onshore and offshore.
