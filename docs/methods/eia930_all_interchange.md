# EIA-930's hourly interchange of every directed pair EIA reports, balancing authorities, regions and neighbours abroad, from 13 September 2026

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), method note written in session 2298 (chain 105) for a table first written on 1 October 2026 (session 49, Part A, which carried out session 42's plan; `archive/sessions/SESSION_49_REPORT.md` and the rerun section of `archive/sessions/SESSION_42_REPORT.md`; commit `6dd3daeb`). The route, the entity and column scheme, the hour-start reading and EIA's sign convention, checked against net generation less demand for the seven ISOs, are in `docs/methods/grid_network.md` ("The source: `eia930_all_interchange`"), with the network built from this table; they are not repeated here. The daily pairs since 2019 are `eia930_daily_interchange` (`docs/methods/eia930_daily_interchange.md`); the hourly pairs of two event windows are `eia930_event_hourly_interchange`.

Tables: `eia930_all_interchange`

## Publisher and address

U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), Form EIA-930 (Hourly Electric Grid Monitor), `https://www.eia.gov/electricity/gridmonitor/`, through EIA's API v2, route `electricity/rto/interchange-data` (`https://api.eia.gov/v2/electricity/rto/interchange-data/data/`), frequency hourly, no facet, one UTC day a request, sorted by period, `fromba` and `toba`, paged by 5,000. The key comes from `EIA_API_KEY` and is replaced by `REDACTED` in every stored URL (all 295,104 rows). Source id `eia:electricity/rto/interchange-data`, which `sources.csv` shares with `eia930_event_hourly_interchange`. Raw files under `warehouse/raw/eia930_interchange/<run_id>/`, day checkpoints under `.../checkpoints/`: none on this machine, and no run log of the connector in `warehouse/output/logs/` (every run the table holds was made on the GitHub runner or, for the first, in session 49).

Connector: `warehouse/connectors/eia930_interchange.py`, `--days N` (the last N complete UTC days, ending at the last UTC midnight). It counts the window's rows with a zero-length request and stops without writing above its ceiling of 150,000 rows, which governs a run's window, not the file. It has its own pager because the shared `eia930.fetch_route` sorts by respondent, which this route rejects (HTTP 400). Days in the last four days of the window are pulled again and never checkpointed. A value that is not a number is dropped; completeness is per pair and UTC day ("a pair-day is written only when all 24 hours have a value"), and the pair-days left out are counted in one gap row per run. The run merges by (entity, variable, ts_utc) into the file (`iso_prices.write_csv`); nothing trims it.

Schedule: the daily run, `warehouse/run_daily.sh`: `pull eia930_interchange "$PYTHON" warehouse/connectors/eia930_interchange.py --days "$DAYS"` (3 days by default), then `run_other grid_network`. GitHub runner, `.github/workflows/daily-prices.yml` (cron `0 14 * * *`, started by pg_cron through `workflow_dispatch`). The table is restored before the run (`warehouse/redivis/config.yaml`, `'^eia930_all_interchange$'`, "session 49: EIA-930 interchange keeps its history by merging, so the grid network always has its 168 hours"). No step of `run_data_machine.sh`, `run_data_machine_tables.sh`, `run_monthly.sh`, `refresh_supply.sh` or `warehouse/scheduled.py` runs it.

`run_status.csv`: 34 rows, all from the GitHub runner, 17 ok and 17 gap, from run `20261001T231023Z` to `20261023T143642Z`. `archive_manifest.csv`: 295,104 rows as of run `20261023T152740Z-github`. Not in the Supabase live set (no rule of `live_set.yaml` names it; `package/llms.txt`: "not in the live set (hourly history)"). Readers: `warehouse/derived/grid_network.py` (`grid_network_links`, `grid_network_nodes` and the snapshot `/network` draws), `warehouse/analysis/eia930_balance.py`, `warehouse/analysis/watch.py`, the chat's tools (`warehouse/chat/tools.py`), `site/lib/gridtables.ts` (the license lines of the Grid tool), `site/lib/gridKeys.ts` (the source lines of the network's figures), `site/lib/problems.ts` (problems e1 and e2) and `package/llms.txt`.

## Shape and variables

Series, 295,104 rows, 15 columns: the series columns, `ba` (the reporting party, lower case) and `x_to_ba` (the neighbour, lower case). One variable, `interchange_mw`, unit MW, `freq` PT1H; `geo`, `market`, `node` and `vintage` blank in every row. Entity `eia930:<FROM>-<TO>` in capitals, equal to `ba` and `x_to_ba` in every row. 345 directed pairs, 74 reporting codes, 85 neighbour codes; 37 days held from 2026-09-13 to 2026-10-21, 7,296 to 8,184 rows a day (304 to 341 pairs; median 335 pairs). Rows a month: 143,544 (September 2026) and 151,560 (October to the 21st).

- **Who reports.** 60 of the reporting codes are balancing authorities; 14 are EIA's regions and the Lower 48 (CAL, CAR, CENT, FLA, MIDA, MIDW, NE, NW, NY, SE, SW, TEN, TEX, US48), which report 39,096 rows over 45 pairs; 31,752 rows have a region as the neighbour. Twelve neighbour codes never report in this window: AESO, BCHA, CAN, CEN, HQT, IESO, MEX, MHEB, NBSO, SPC, WAUW and WWA. A sum over all rows counts a flow again in each region and in US48.
- **Pairs over time.** Five pairs begin after the first day: PJM to CPLW on 2026-09-14, and CENT to CAN, SWPP to SIKE, SWPP to SPA and SWPP to SPC on 2026-10-07. Four end on 2026-10-09: SPA to AECI, MISO, SIKE and SWPP. Most pairs are held on all 37 days (the median); the fewest days a pair is 13.
- **Sign.** Positive when the reporting party exports to the neighbour (the header; `grid_network.md` for the check). 133,392 pair-hours are reported from both ends: on 77,994 the two values are exactly opposite, on 100,426 within 5 percent (or 1 MW) of opposite, and the median of their sum is 0; on 4,488 both ends report the same sign. 28,320 rows, over 144 directed pairs, have no report from the other end in that hour; 18,168 of them because the neighbour is one of the twelve that never report.
- Values: 136,859 positive, 144,642 negative, 13,603 zero; every value a whole number. Half the values are within 197 MW of zero, 99 percent within 3,707 MW.
- **Values no tie can carry,** held as EIA reports them: four hours near minus 2.15 million MW, SWPP to MISO at 2026-10-04T12:00Z (-2,148,410 MW) and 2026-10-06T19:00Z (-2,148,400 MW), and CENT to MIDW in the same two hours (-2,148,660 and -2,148,620 MW). The other end reported -55 MW (MISO to SWPP) and 93 MW (MIDW to CENT) in the first hour, and 837 MW (MISO to SWPP) in the second; SWPP's report of the hours either side of the first is -770 and -820 MW. The next largest value is 9,697 MW (SEC to FPC, 2026-10-16T19:00Z). A sum over SWPP's or CENT's ties in those hours carries the value.

## Placing values in time, and what is filled

`ts_utc` is the start of the hour in UTC (EIA's `period` is the end of the hour; one hour is subtracted). Completeness is judged on UTC days: all 12,296 pair-days held have 24 hours, and the rows are exactly 24 times the pair-days. Nothing is filled: a pair-day with a missing hour, or one EIA does not report, has no row.

**Two days have no row at all: 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15.** The run of 15 October wrote 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-13; no run pulled the table again until 19 October, whose three-day window began on 2026-10-16. No row of `run_status.csv` names the two days: the runs of 15 and 19 October have no row for the table there.

## Where the description and the data disagree

1. **The session.** The connector's docstring reads "connector, session 46 (Part A, session 42's prompt A1)". Session 46 made no pull ("0 rows against the 150,000 (interchange) ... ceilings"); the connector and the table are session 49's (commit `6dd3daeb`, `SESSION_49_REPORT.md`), and its usage line's "--days 30 # the approved pull" was run as 18 days.
2. **"Between balancing authorities ... every pair EIA reports."** Header line 2, the docstring's title and `package/llms.txt` call the table interchange between balancing authorities. 14 of the 74 reporting codes are EIA's regions and the Lower 48 (39,096 rows), and twelve neighbours never report: ten Canadian and Mexican parties (AESO, BCHA, CAN, CEN, HQT, IESO, MEX, MHEB, NBSO, SPC) and two US balancing authorities (WAUW, WWA).
3. **"Each pair is reported by both BAs."** The docstring ("Each pair is reported by both BAs, so the table holds both directions") and `package/llms.txt` ("Both BAs of a pair report it"). 28,320 rows over 144 directed pairs have no report from the other end in the hour, 18,168 because the neighbour never reports.
4. **"A rolling window."** The docstring of `warehouse/connectors/eia930_event_hourly.py` ("a rolling window of recent weeks"), `docs/methods/eia930_daily_total_interchange.md` ("a rolling window") and `docs/methods/eia930_event_hourly_interchange.md` ("The warehouse's rolling hourly interchange"). Nothing trims the table: it holds every day since 2026-09-13 and grows by a day a day (`grid_network.md` and `llms.txt`, "grown daily", are right).
5. **Coverage names seven grids.** `coverage.csv` gives the table's ISOs as "CAISO;ERCOT;ISO-NE;MISO;NYISO;PJM;SPP"; it holds 345 pairs across the Lower 48, EIA's regions and the neighbours abroad.
6. **"Each ... is a gap row."** The header's completeness line and the docstring say the pair-days not written are counted in a gap row of `run_status.csv`. 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15 are missing whole and no row names them; the table holds rows of session 49's pull and of runs of 3, 15 and 19 October that have no row in `run_status.csv`.
7. **"18 days, 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-30."** Session 49's commit, `SESSION_49_REPORT.md` and `grid_network.md` ("the latest 18 complete UTC days were pulled, 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-30") give the window asked for. The 127,560 rows written cover 16 days, 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-28 (the rerun section of `SESSION_42_REPORT.md` says so: "Data runs through 2026-09-28 at the time of the pull"); 29 and 30 September came from later daily runs.

Not acted on: this note changes no file but itself. The four impossible hours and the two missing days are recorded, not changed.
