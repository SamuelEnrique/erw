# Cross-checks: What a battery earns (`/cost-of-power/battery`)

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW). Session 3707, chain 208, 6 November 2026. Roadmap item 2 asks each of the five tools for "a cross-check table against published figures". This file sets **each headline figure the page prints** against a figure a named publisher states. The headline figures are the 22 of `replication/battery/page_figures.json`: the default case, ERCOT, 100 MW, 4 hours, at the hub average `HB_HUBAVG`, perfect foresight, the last twelve months October 2025 to September 2026.

- **Where the published figures come from.** Each is quoted from the method note's "Cross-checks" (`docs/methods/battery_earns_algorithm.md`), which says when each was read, after the host's robots file and terms. The exception is the row on the fleet's fall from 2024 to 2025: ERCOT's market monitor's 2025 report, read by session 3708 on 6 November 2026 (its robots file allows every path but `/wp-admin/`). It is quoted, not copied.
- **The columns.** "Gap" is ours less theirs, in our figure's unit. "Reason" is one sentence, or "not explained". A figure with no published counterpart is said to have none.

## The largest gaps, in one line each

- **2024, USD per kW: +92.68 against Modo's fleet average.** Perfect foresight, reserves never called, 4 hours at the hub, against a fleet of mostly shorter batteries. The E3 row, the closest method, is +20.68.
- **2025, USD per kW: +58.04 against Modo's nowcast.** The same reasons. At the fleet's own duration, 2 hours (the note's session 464 row), ours is 56.01 on the day-ahead schedule, 1.9 times theirs.
- **The breakeven toll: 7.89 to 22.48 per kW-month below Lazard's levelized cost.** The toll pays the debt and fixed O&M; Lazard's cost pays everything, including a return on equity.

## Not compared, and why

- Fourteen of the 22 figures have no published counterpart. Each is the page's own arithmetic, a count, or one battery's figure that no publisher states.
- The capital cost, O&M, efficiency, life, cycles and cost of capital are assumptions, not headline figures. The note's "Cross-checks" sets each against Lazard and the ATB.
- CAISO's figures (the note's rows against CAISO's Department of Market Monitoring) are not of the default case.
