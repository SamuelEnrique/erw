# The independent audit of utilities_2026-11-13.csv (sessions 5302 to 5304)

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), portable laptop window P4, 14 November 2026.

Five auditor subagents read all 122 rows. None had read or written the rows. Each worked only from the cached copies, with no web request, and judged every value, status and date against its quoted words in place: "ok", "wrong" (the value misstates the words) or "unclear" (the words could support it but context is missing). Their reports are in `audit/` (one JSON a batch).

Every finding is resolved below. A correction is applied with `fix_field.py`, which writes nothing unless the new quote is on its cited page. A correction made directly in the rows (a date cleared, a status changed on cites the row already holds) is re-checked with `verify_quotes.py`. Each corrected row keeps the old value in its notes, with the finding's number.

## Errors in this session's own corrections, found by the later batches

- **A-06's appendix letters.** For northeast-04, -08 and -09 I wrote "Appendix B" where the reports are Appendices A, F and C. Batch 4 found it (A-37 to A-39), and the letters and the A-05 notes now name each row's own appendix.
- **A-05's cleared date on northeast-07.** National Grid's Appendix D is the one appendix of the DPS report that prints a date, "Date: September 18, 2026" (p. 79). The first auditor had read it as supported. The status date is now 2026-09-18, cited to that page.
