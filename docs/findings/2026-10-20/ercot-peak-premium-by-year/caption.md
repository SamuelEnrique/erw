The peak premium. ERCOT, the hub average: the year's median evening-peak real-time price minus its median midday price at the hub average (ercot_peak_premium_annual), 14.52 USD per MWh in 2025, up from -0.16 USD per MWh in 2015. The direction holds in 10 of the 10 complete years after 2015. The evening charges what the midday no longer does.

Checked five ways before it was drafted: the direction holds in 10 of the 10 later years; it survives a change of window; 99 percent of the change remains without the first year; the move is 7.4 typical yearly moves of the series; both ends hold at least 35,040 observations.

The claim states a change; it does not say what caused it.

Source: ercot_peak_premium_annual, as the ERW holds it. ERW table: ercot_peak_premium_annual.
