### The four rows marked `ruling`, in plain words, with the default each stands on

1. **Row 1: do the facilities stay held whole?** No reader, the internal view included, sees any of the 458 facilities. The other choice is to show the rows whose every source is public, each with its license. **Default: held whole.**
2. **Row 4: do each grid's peak hour and capacity show while the facilities are held?** Those two columns are EIA's public data; they read "license needed" only because their keys sit with the held table's. **Default: hidden, with the hold's footnote.**
3. **Row 7: may the map draw the facilities the tracker holds?** The map sends the placed facilities' names, operators and megawatts to every reader's browser while the tracker shows none. **Default: the map unchanged.** One of the two is wrong until this is ruled.
4. **Row 11: does every figure on the site show its source on hover?** A figure carries its check key; its source is a line under its table. The change reaches every page of the site. **Default: unchanged.**
