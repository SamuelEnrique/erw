**Not a fault, noted.**

- Seattle City Light's Business Rates page prints no effective date for its rate tables; the cases cite the page as read on 16 November 2026 and its line that a 4 percent surcharge was removed on April 1, 2026.
- The year shown is twelve times the month before rounding, so it can differ by a few dollars from twelve times the month shown (USD 5,608 a month and USD 67,302 a year in the browser proof's case). Both are inside half a dollar of the arithmetic.
- The kWh a day in "From shifting energy" is printed to the whole kWh, so a 13.5 kWh battery reads "14 kWh a day out" there and "13.5 kWh" in the summary. It is a computed figure at the page's precision, inside half its last digit; left.
- The Southern California Edison case is a rate of 2013 as a report prints it, kept only because the cross-check's report used it. It is not a rate in force.
