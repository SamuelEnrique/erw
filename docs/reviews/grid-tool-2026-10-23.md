- One address, `/grid?grid=<grid>&view=<tab>` (`site/lib/gridtool.ts`). Eight grids (the seven ISOs and the Lower 48), four tabs: Network, Mix (the default), Clean, Gravity. Ask is a panel on the right of every tab. `/network` redirects to `/grid?view=network`.

## What a trader asks, and where the page answers

1. **Is this live?** The demand is one to two hours behind; the mix a day or more; the exchange about two days (said under the network, twice since session 299). The hourly mix chart does not say its last hour's age on its face.
2. **Where is each number from?** Every headline number of the Mix tab is a key with its table; the source line names the tables. The hourly mix chart, the energy mix views drawn from committed files, the Clean tab's "How clean, and when" and the whole network component carry no key: a reader cannot trace them from the page, and check-values does not check them.
3. **When was the grid short?** Nowhere on the page. The scarcity table is held and not drawn; CAISO's notices are a table in the CAISO Mix tab; ERCOT's notices (`ercot_grid_notices`) are not drawn either.
4. **Who supplies this grid?** The Gravity tab's two blocks answer it with keys; the shape itself has none.
5. **What does the infrastructure lead need?** What is being built (the queue, keyed), the batteries (keyed), how tight the evenings were (CAISO only), and how the grid's mix moves through the day: the last is drawn from committed files, keyless, and stops at the month the file was built.

## What this chain fixes, ranked

Ranked by how much a reader's trust in a number moves, then by size.

1. **The network component's figures keyed** (session 1151). Every number the Network and Gravity tabs show in the browser gets a server-side twin a reader can trace and check-values can check: the selected grid's ties, net exchange and demand in the hour shown, as `grid_network_<grid>` keys, each with a one-line source in its tip. Without it the busiest tab of the tool has one key.
2. **The hourly mix keyed, its age said, its peak marked** (session 1152). The 7-day chart's headline figures (the last 24 hours' share by fuel, the day's peak hour) become keys `grid_mix_<grid>`; the last interval's age on the face; the monthly mix as the second view; CAISO stays withheld by the session 118 ruling (that is not a license hold, so it is said as the ruling, not as "license needed").
3. **The scarcity hours drawn** (session 1154). `scarcity_hours_by_grid` as a strip by day and hour for ERCOT and CAISO, the latest 30 days the table holds (to 1 October 2026), the count and the highest price on hover, the USD 1,000 line and the own signal named in the tip with the method; the other five grids "not held" or "license needed" by their own reason.
4. **The gravity view's honesty** (session 1153). Every edge's MW in the hour on hover with its window, the importer-to-centre rule said on the face, the day in the address; no edge without a figure.
5. **CAISO's alerts tied in** (session 1155). The CAISO row of `/grid` says whether a notice is in force today (from `caiso_grid_emergencies`), links `/grid/caiso/alerts`, and marks alert days on the scarcity strip and the mix chart.
6. **The map's flows on hover** (session 1156), within the map component as it is; a new dependency is not added.
7. **Ask on stored answers** (session 1157): five stored example answers by grid, no model call; the live box is chain 075's.
8. **390 pixels, accessibility, links** (session 1158), **the brand pass** (1159), **the reads timed** (1160), **the trader's path again** (1161), **the method notes and the rulings list** (1162).
