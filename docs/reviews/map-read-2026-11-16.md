# The project map (`/map`) read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part C, session 7537, 16 November 2026)

**The reader:** a developer checking what is planned within 30 miles of a candidate site: generating units EIA lists as planned, queue positions, and datacenters, by name, size, status and date.
**The five cases:** (1) a 200 MW battery near Abilene, Texas (32.4487, -99.7331; Taylor County, ERCOT); (2) a 300 MW datacenter near Amarillo, Texas (35.2220, -101.8313); (3) a 200 MW battery near New Albany, Ohio (40.0812, -82.8088; PJM); (4) a 150 MW wind farm near Mason City, Iowa (43.1536, -93.2010; MISO); (5) a 100 MW battery near Bakersfield, California (35.3733, -119.0187; Kern County, CAISO). Each within 30 miles, on EIA's inventory of 2026-09, the queues of 2026-11-05 and the tracker of 2026-11-15 (the page's file as built 16 November 2026).
**How it was read:** this machine cannot build the site. The Projects tab was drawn to HTML by its own component on the page's own file for each case's address (`site/scripts/map-read-page.mjs`, new), every distance computed again by the test's own formula (`site/scripts/test-map-read.mjs`, 42 of 42), and read line by line with every tip. Not seen: no click, no hover in a browser, not the drawing (a box with no marks without a browser), no layout at 390 px.

Part A's rows (`docs/reviews/map-faults-2026-11-16.md`) are cited by number and not written again.

## What this reader still cannot get from the tool, and why

- **A queue position's site and planned date.** A position is at its county's or town's point (the queues name a county), and its only date is its entry into the queue (row 12).
- **One project once.** A planned unit and its queue position are two rows (row 8).
- **MISO's, NYISO's and PJM's queues.** Not drawn (MISO paused, NYISO no license, PJM not held); their planned generating units are, from EIA (row 3 says so where it matters).
- **A place by name.** Coordinates only (row 9).
- **Anything since EIA's month 2026-09, and small projects.** Units under 1 MW at plants under EIA's threshold are not in the inventory (the note's section 15).
