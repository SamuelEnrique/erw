# Curtailment (`/curtailment`) read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part C, session 7544, 16 November 2026)

**The reader:** a solar developer or a battery developer in CAISO or ERCOT sizing how much energy is curtailed, when, at what price, and what it would be worth to store it. Not a planner of the whole grid, and not a trader: every curtailment figure on the page is the whole system's (CAISO publishes no place; ERCOT's limit is system-wide), and prices are the hubs' and zones'.
**The five cases:** (1) CAISO, April 2026, the highest month held: how much solar was curtailed, in which hours, for which reason, and the share; (2) CAISO, January to October 2026 and October 2026 alone, at SP15 and NP15: what the curtailed energy would have fetched, and how much of it fell in hours priced below zero; (3) a 4-hour against an 8-hour battery, April 2026 and October 2026: how much curtailed energy each could have taken in a day, and at what price; (4) ERCOT, October 2026, the one whole month held, at HB WEST: how much output stood below the limit, and at what price; (5) the same months on `/shoulder`, `/storage` and `/cost-of-power/battery`, which share figures with this page.
**How it was read:** this machine cannot build the site. The whole page was drawn to HTML by the page's own function for each case's address (`site/scripts/curtailment-page.mjs`, new: the page's two reads of the live set answered from this machine's tables, everything else from the site's own files, `site/data/curtailment/` built 16 November 2026 19:05 UTC and `site/data/curtailment_profile.json` built 18:25 UTC), and read line by line with every hover. Twelve addresses: the default, `period=2025`, `2026-04`, `2026-10`, `2026-10&dur=8`, `2025-05&dur=2`, `grid=ercot`, `grid=spp`, and four after the fixes. Not seen: no click, no chart, no hover in a browser, no layout at 390 px, and not the live set's rows (the worktree has no key; the two daily tables of this machine stop at 4 November 2026, so the "By day" sections are this machine's, not the site's).

Part A's rows (`docs/reviews/curtailment-faults-2026-11-16.md`, three rows) are cited by number and not written again.
