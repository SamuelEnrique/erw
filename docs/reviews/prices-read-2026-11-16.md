# Power prices (`/prices`, its first tab, and `/prices/<hub>`) read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part C, session 7539, 16 November 2026)

**The reader:** a fund analyst or a merchant developer comparing hub prices, their peaks and their spread across grids and months, on Monday 16 November 2026, with the page drawn at 23:20 UTC.
**The five cases:** (1) ERCOT's West hub against its Houston hub, day-ahead, October 2026: each hub's month average, its highest hour, and West less Houston; (2) CAISO's SP15 against NP15, real time, over the page's 30 days (17 October to 16 November 2026): each hub's highest 15-minute price and when it fell; (3) three grids' main hubs side by side, ERCOT's Hub Average, CAISO's SP15 and SPP's North hub, day-ahead, October 2026 (and September 2026, the month the site holds); (4) ERCOT's Hub Average, real time against day-ahead over the page's 30 days: the average spread and the widest hours; (5) New York City (NYISO Zone J), ISO-NE's Internal Hub and MISO's Indiana Hub over the same 30 days.
**How it was read:** this machine cannot build the site. Both pages were drawn whole to HTML by their own functions (`site/scripts/prices-read-page.mjs`, new) on the rows the live set gave the public key at 23:20 UTC (ERCOT, CAISO and SPP only; no held grid's row was saved), at that render time, and read cell by cell with every hover. The cases' answers off the page were computed from the warehouse's own tables of this machine (`iso_dam_hub_prices`, `iso_rtm_hub_prices`, to 13 November 2026) and from `site/data/price_compare.json`. Not seen: no click, no chart, no hover in a browser, no layout at 390 px. The ERCOT tab (the peak premium explorer) is the read of `/explore/ercot-peak-premium` (session 7507's tool) and was not read again; `/prices/compare` is its own read.

The tool's done-line files are cited and not written again: `docs/methods/power_prices.md` (the note, its cross-checks and its 8 decisions; this session added the section "A hub's page, as read by session 7539"), `replication/prices/` (74,258 of 74,258) and section 18 of `docs/reviews/desktop-tools-signature-pack-2026-11-16.md`.
