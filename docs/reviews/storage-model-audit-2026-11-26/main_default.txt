Decisions on this page (each with its default and its source on hover) Battery output is net generation as published: positive while the batteries discharge to the grid, negative while they charge, so a day's charging is the part below zero taken as a positive amount. (session 31) i Default: Positive discharging, negative charging. Source: EIA-930's battery series (fuel type BAT) and CAISO's Today's Outlook, both signed this way. EIA's hourly period is the hour's end in UTC, so each hour is dated by its start, one hour before EIA's stamp; CAISO's hour is the mean of its twelve 5-minute values. (session 31) i Default: Every hour dated by its start. Source: EIA API v2, electricity/rto/fuel-type-data; CAISO's 5-minute battery history. A day is the balancing authority's local day and counts only when every hour of it is held (23 or 25 on the clock-change days; for CAISO every 5-minute interval); a day short of one is left out, never filled. (session 31) i Default: Whole local days only. Source: docs/methods/storage.md, "Days" and "Completeness". Each authority's day is in its own time: Central for ERCOT and SPP, Pacific for CAISO, Eastern for ISO-NE and for the Lower 48 (as EIA's Grid Monitor shows it), and fixed EST for MISO, as MISO publishes. (session 31) i Default: Each grid's own local time; MISO on fixed EST. Source: The operators' market time zones; EIA's Grid Monitor default. The day's energy out over energy in is not a measured efficiency: charge held across midnight moves it, and so do batteries reported under other fuels (solar or wind with integrated storage). (session 31) i Default: Shown as out over in, labelled not an efficiency. Source: docs/methods/storage.md, the daily cycle's notes. California is drawn from CAISO's own 5-minute battery data, not EIA-930's, which CAISO does not report as BAT; it includes the batteries of hybrid plants, which CAISO publishes inside its total. (session 34) i Default: CAISO's own data for California, labelled as CAISO's. Source: CAISO, Today's Outlook, battery storage history ("Total batteries"). The fleet is every generator with EIA's prime mover "Batteries" in EIA-860M, at its nameplate MW and, where EIA gives it, its nameplate energy; the page's sums by status, grid, state and planned year are sums of those rows and nothing else. (session 31) i Default: EIA-860M battery units at nameplate; sums of the rows only. Source: EIA-860M, Monthly Update to the Annual Electric Generator Report. Energy capacity in MWh is EIA's own figure, carried for operating and retired units; planned units have none in EIA-860M, and a unit's MWh is never estimated from its MW. (session 34) i Default: MWh only where EIA states it. Source: EIA-860M, "Nameplate Energy Capacity (MWh)". A unit's grid is the ISO of its balancing authority when that is one of the seven; any other unit stands outside the ISOs with its own authority's code, never assigned by its state. (session 31) i Default: The ISO of the unit's balancing authority, else outside the ISOs. Source: EIA-860M's balancing authority code for each generator.
