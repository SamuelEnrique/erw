## What to look at first

1. **The datacenter page's cost of capital** (`site/lib/datacenter/finance.ts:85`): 9.6 percent, labelled "a generator's WACC, Lazard", is 0.6 x 8 + 0.4 x 12, a pre-tax weighting. The figure Lazard itself calls the WACC of that structure is **7.7 percent**, after tax (page 9 note; page 12, the column at 8 percent debt and 12 percent equity). The gap is 1.9 points. Changing either the label or the default is a ruling for Samuel.
2. **The battery's sourced sensitivity says the tax credit has "none published as a range"** (`site/lib/battery/finance.ts:548`), but Lazard's page 43, the page every other storage default comes from, prints an investment tax credit for storage of **30 to 40 percent**. The default of no credit is not in question; the words in the sourced mode are wrong.
3. **The 8-hour battery mixes dollar years.** Its capital cost, USD 2,300 per kW, is the ATB cost report's 2024 dollars; the ATB workbook the warehouse holds gives USD 2,137.36 for the Moderate case in 2023 dollars (+162.64, 7.6 percent). The 2- and 4-hour costs are Lazard's 2025 midpoints. No figure is wrong, but the page sets the three durations side by side.
4. **The generator page's peaker runs 24.0 percent of the hours in ERCOT in 2024; Lazard's peaker runs 10 to 15 percent** (page 37). The page's rule runs it whenever the price clears fuel and variable cost, with no start cost or minimum run. The CAISO rows already in the note show the same against CAISO's own study.
5. **ERCOT's solar fleet ran at 29.5 percent over the last twelve months; Lazard's ERCOT solar plant at 24 percent** (page 30), its utility plant at 20 to 30 percent, and the ATB at 19.4 to 31.5 percent. The fleet sits inside both ranges and 5.5 points above Lazard's regional figure.

## Not compared, and why

- **Lazard's subsidized LCOE bars (page 9)** print two values a technology (with and without the Energy Community adder), and the text of the PDF gives them in an order that cannot be matched to the bars with certainty. Not used. The unsubsidized ranges of page 8 are used.
- **Lazard's regional LCOE with firming (page 30)**: the regional capacity factors, ELCC and Net CONE are used (their table reads unambiguously); the bars' dollar values per region are not, for the same reason as page 9.
- **The ATB's figures for a gas combustion turbine** (capital, O&M, heat rate) are in its interactive chart, not in the text of its fossil page (request 8). Not read. The ATB workbook would hold them; it was not requested (a workbook of the data machine's, sources.csv `nlr:atb-2025-v1`), and the request is left for that machine.
- **Lazard's 2-hour LCOS** (USD 129 to 277 per MWh, page 19; USD 81 to 174 per kW-year, page 20) has no ERW figure beside it. The page's 2-hour breakeven toll is not in any note or report read here, and computing it was not this session's work.
- **Lazard's charging cost** (USD 33 per MWh at 2 hours, 27 at 4 hours, page 43): the page charges at each hour's price and states no yearly mean.
- **Lazard's gas combined cycle LCOE** (USD 48 to 109 per MWh, page 8; 41 to 116 with fuel at plus or minus 25 percent, page 10): the datacenter page computes no cost of its own generation, so nothing stands beside it. It is the figure a future "build your own power" choice would be checked against.
- **One request of the ceiling was not spent.**
