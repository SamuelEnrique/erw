: Texas, oil, a monthly volume of 1,000 at the latest price shown, no deductions.

Every tax and fee here is in proportion to the volume: the whole well's or lease's month gives the whole tax, and one owner's share of the volume (a one-eighth royalty: the month's volume divided by 8) gives the tax on that share.Who bears the tax, the operator or each owner, is not a rule this page has read.
