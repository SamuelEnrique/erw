## How

- **Each chart is drawn again from its own draft:** the card the builder stored (public.scanner_drafts, read only), the line, the marked point and the first year's line read again from the draft's CSV, and the line under the title taken from the draft's `.md` as it stands. Before a picture is written, every figure it prints is set against the CSV and the sentence; a mismatch writes nothing. The tool: `warehouse/analysis/findings/deep/redraw.py` (code on `wip/9820-findings-charts`, for the landing desk).
- **What was wrong is measured on the picture as it was drawn:** the old drawing code (main's `card_image.py`) renders the card again and its labels, legend, lines and axis are read back: a label whose box crosses the line, a legend over the data, zero on the scale with no ink zero line, years missing from the table drawn one step apart, the marked year without a label, a printed figure that differs from the sentence, text past the picture's edge. The forty of the sheet were also opened and looked at, before and after.
- **The fixes are in the chart code** (`warehouse/analysis/findings/card_image.py`), one change for all drafts:
  1. the marked figure, the first year's label and each series' name are placed where they cross no line, point or other label (each candidate place tried in order; a render that finds no clear place says so);
  2. a chart with two series names each on its line, in its own color, as `docs/brand.md` section 6 asks; no legend;
  3. the zero line in ink when zero is on the scale (section 6);
  4. years at their own places: a year the table does not hold is a gap in the line, not a step;
  5. axis labels counted back from the last year, so the marked year is always labelled (the first too when a step divides the span); the events' days every k-th day back from the last;
  6. titles, sentences and source lines wrapped by their measured width, and a cut said, not silent;
  7. the figures drawn from the CSV at full precision (two pictures printed 28.43 and 404.8 where their sentences say 28.42 and 404.7).
- **Colors:** every color drawn is one of `docs/brand.md`: page, ink, cardinal (the marked point, the highlight), captions grey, rule, the chart blue for a second series. None was outside the brand file; the missing ink zero line was the one color rule broken.
- **Not done:** the square 1080 px pictures and the copies inside each Supabase row keep the old drawing (no write to Supabase); the covid-2020 event drafts (a low point, not a peak) are not drawn by this tool and keep their pictures.
