writing the method note's done-line section, the replication (`replication/battery_game/`, 3,304 of 3,304 with the
tables) and the cross-check (`docs/reviews/battery-game-crosscheck-2026-11-16.md`). One row a fault, with the cell or
click it faults. A site fault is fixed with a test (`site/scripts/test-battery-game-render.mjs`, 85 of 85, and
`tests/test_session7535_battery_game.py`); a data fault is not fixed here and goes to the data machine; a ruling says
the default taken in plain words.

rows and the game's own fleet), so the known fault of the hold (figures whose key names no grid, sessions 7531, 7533,
7534, 7537) does not reach this page. The game's scores carry no key.
