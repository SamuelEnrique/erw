Email ERW's Energy Digest and ERW's Roundup by email, as short plain text with an HTML copy. What the email is Weekdays (no weekend issue): the day's Energy Digest in short: its opening number; its numbers (a market price, a physical figure such as demand, a project, and a policy, capital or reliability figure); the first three stories of what moved, each with why it matters and a link to its source; the chart of the day and a fun fact. The rest of the issue is one link away on the site. Sundays, 23:00 UTC (4 PM Pacific in summer time, 3 PM in winter): the ERW's Roundup : its opening number, the week in five numbers, the first three stories of what mattered, the chart of the week, the week ahead and a fun fact; the weekend's stories and the rest of the week are one link away on the site. Each figure says where it came from: a warehouse table, as on this site, or, for a figure a news story reported, its outlet. The email links back to the site for everything else. Your topics: with every topic chosen, the email's stories are the first three of the issue's own; with fewer, up to five of the day's (or week's) highest-ranked stories in the topics you choose. The numbers, the chart and the fun fact are always included. The website digest stays complete.
