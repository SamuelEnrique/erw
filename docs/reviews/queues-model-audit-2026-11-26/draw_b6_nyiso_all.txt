The interconnection queue
Before a power plant or a battery can connect to the grid it waits in a queue for a study and an agreement. Choose a grid and a technology: how much capacity is waiting and since when, what became of the requests of earlier years, and how long the ones that were built took. From Berkeley Lab and GridTracker's record of every request, through the end of 2025. See also the storage build-out and the project map . The grid operators' own queue lists, as the ERW pulls them, are counted on the interconnection queue explorer and the map's Queues tab : another source, read on another day, so their count for a grid and an entry year is not this page's.

What the file does not hold
Requests after the end of 2025: the file is a yearly edition, not a live queue.
One date for every grid. The 2026 edition is Berkeley Lab's of the end of 2025, but each grid's newest dates in it differ: it holds no CAISO request after 17 April 2023 and one ISO-NE request of 2025; NYISO's newest operation date is 31 December 2023 and MISO's 19 December 2024, so a later year has no point for them because the file dates none, not because none was built. And its statuses run past the year's end: 53 requests (13,275 MW) carry a withdrawal date of 2 to 16 January 2026 and count as withdrawn, not active.
Every plant that operates. A request is "operational" here only when its operator's queue says so; operators differ in how long they keep finished and withdrawn requests, so a share is of what the queue records, not of everything ever proposed.
The operation date of many built requests: of 4,789 that reached operation, 3,098 have both a request date and an operation date in order. ISO-NE's have none, so it has no median wait.
The year entered of 665 requests, and the status of 10.
The storage part of a hybrid on its own: a solar-with-storage request carries one total here.
Whether an active request will be built. Most are not: that is what the shares above measure for earlier years.
Cost, the developer, the network upgrades asked for, or the reason a request was withdrawn.
The same project twice: a developer may file at several points for one plant, and each request counts.
A load. Every request here is a power plant or a battery asking to connect; a data center's or any other large customer's request to draw power is in no edition of the file, and no wait here is a load's. ERCOT's large loads (MW approved to energize, report by report) are on the datacenters page ; the All grids tab of the cost of power sets each grid's wait for generators beside the large-load rules the ERW has read.
How it is computed
Counts and sums of the file's rows, grouped by Berkeley Lab's region and by its clean technology label. A past request entered from 2000 to 2020 and has a known status. A share is given when at least 20 past requests are counted, a median when at least 5 requests are dated. Nothing is estimated. The full method: the interconnection queue, summarized .

Every figure is from Berkeley Lab's yearly Queued Up data file, one row a request through the end of 2025; a newer request is not in it, and the next edition replaces every figure. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: The 2026 edition, through 2025. Source: Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory and GridTracker, Queued Up: 2026 Edition data file (CC BY 4.0).
A request is counted once, under the region of the queue that holds it: the seven ISOs, Berkeley Lab's West and Southeast outside them, and the United States as every request of the file. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: Berkeley Lab's region; the US as every request. Source: The data file's region field; docs/methods/interconnection_queue_summary.md, "Grids and technologies".
Technologies follow Berkeley Lab's cleaned type: solar, solar with storage, storage alone, onshore wind, offshore wind and gas each on its own, and every other type and hybrid in one "other"; storage alone and solar with storage are kept apart from solar. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: Seven groups from the cleaned type, hybrids apart. Source: The data file's cleaned type field (type_clean).
A request's MW is the sum of its parts as filed (all parts of a hybrid together); a request with no positive capacity is counted and adds no MW. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: Every part's MW summed; none filled. Source: The data file's capacity of each part.
Status is Berkeley Lab's: active, suspended, operational and withdrawn; a request of unknown status is counted as entered and in no status, and suspended requests are not in the active figures. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: Berkeley Lab's four statuses; unknown counted as entered only. Source: The data file's queue status field.
Completion and withdrawal shares are taken over requests that entered from 2000 to five years before the edition's last year, with a known status, so recent years, still mostly open, do not read as a low completion rate. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: Entered 2000 to 2020 for the 2026 edition. Source: docs/methods/interconnection_queue_summary.md, "Past requests".
A share is written only from at least 20 past requests and a median only from at least 5 dated ones; below that the cell reads "not held", since one project would move a smaller share by five points or more. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: 20 requests for a share; 5 for a median. Source: docs/methods/interconnection_queue_summary.md, "When a figure is not written".
Years to operation are the days from the request to the operation date over 365.25, for requests that reached operation with both dates in order; a request dated the other way round is left out, and ISO-NE, whose operating requests carry no date, has no median. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: Dated requests only, both dates in order. Source: The data file's request and operation dates.
The figures are of what the queues record: operators keep finished and withdrawn requests for different lengths of time, and the file holds no cost, developer, network upgrade or reason for a withdrawal, and no forecast of what will be built. (session 95) i
[tip] Default: What the queues record, nothing inferred. Source: docs/methods/interconnection_queue_summary.md, "What the table does not hold".
