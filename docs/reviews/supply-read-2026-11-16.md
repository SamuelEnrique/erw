# Supply and trade (`/supply`) read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part C, session 7541, 16 November 2026)

**The reader:** a utility planner or a fund analyst reading what fuels a grid's supply, what the country imports and exports, and how each moved over named weeks and months, on Monday 16 November 2026, with the page's file built at 12:00 UTC that day (`site/data/supply.json`).
**The five cases:** (1) ERCOT, the week ending Friday 6 November 2026: gas and coal burned for power against the same week of 2025 and the five years before, and Texas's whole mix and its trade with its neighbours; (2) ISO-NE going into winter: gas, oil and coal burned for power, the week ending 30 October 2026, and New England's day-ahead demand; (3) PJM against MISO: gas and coal burned for power, the newest week each holds, against a year before; (4) US natural gas exports, August 2026 against July 2026 and August 2025: LNG by terminal (Sabine Pass, Plaquemines, Golden Pass) and pipeline exports to Mexico, beside the price board's figures of the same months; (5) US crude and product trade, the week ending 6 November 2026 against the same week of 2025: imports, exports and net imports.
**How it was read:** this machine cannot build the site. The page was drawn whole to HTML by its own function (`site/app/supply/page.tsx`, through `site/scripts/supply-read-page.mjs`, new) on the site's own file after the day's license hold, and read cell by cell with every hover. Every figure on the page is in `site/data/supply.json`; the page reads no live table. Not seen: no click, no chart, no hover in a browser, no layout at 390 px.

The tool's done-line files are cited and not written again: `docs/methods/supply_and_trade.md` (the note, its cross-checks and its 11 decisions), `replication/supply/` (`replicate.py` and `replicate.do`) and section 20 of `docs/reviews/desktop-tools-signature-pack-2026-11-16.md`. Chain 301 wrote no faults file for `/supply`.

A first session (7541, stalled at 15:46) left the page and lib changes of rows 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 (commit `53624d72c`) and four scratch scripts; this session wrote the rows from those, finished the fixes and wrote the test.

## What a planner or an analyst still cannot get from the page, and why

- **New England's and New York's own figures:** held for a license (rows 1 and 13). A permission, not work, opens them.
- **A grid's newest week for every grid at once:** four grids hold the week ending 6 Nov 2026 and three do not (rows 6 and 8); the total waits for the slowest.
- **A clean year of weekly burn:** 17 weeks of the last 52 are missing from the seven grids' total (row 9).
- **The week's change in day-ahead demand:** two weeks missing in every public grid's table (row 10), and ERCOT three weeks behind (row 16).
- **Months after August 2026 for gas trade and basin production:** EIA's monthly tables end there on the day; the page shows what they hold.
