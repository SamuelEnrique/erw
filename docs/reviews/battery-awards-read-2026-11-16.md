**The reader:** a lender's analyst testing a developer's revenue case for an ERCOT battery against what the fleet was really awarded. Not the merchant developer choosing between two nodes: the page has no input, every figure is the whole fleet's, and it names no settlement point (case 3 says how far it takes that reader).
**The five cases:** (1) July 2026: what a kW of ERCOT storage was awarded day-ahead, and how much of it was ancillary services, by service; (2) July 2026 and January to July 2026: how far the ERW's 2-hour model stands from the fleet's real award, and which of the gap's three parts is largest; (3) two settlement points as ERCOT names them, `AZURE_RN` and `KNAPP_RN`, over the 21 days of node prices held (28 September to 18 October 2026): the spread at each against the hub; (4) January to July 2026: the share of the energy the fleet offered day-ahead that was priced at USD 100 per MWh or less; (5) a 4-hour battery in ERCOT in 2026: what the page says and does not say.

## What this reader still cannot get from the tool, and why

- **What a battery earned.** Every figure is the fleet's award or dispatch valued by the ERW: day-ahead awards, then real time at the hub's price. Contracts, real-time ancillary money, the statement's charges and the meter are in no public file (the page says each).
- **Any month after August 2026, and August whole.** The disclosure is no longer pulled (ERCOT's robots file; the owner's ruling 2 of 6 November 2026), and on this machine the monthly tables stop at 5 August (part A, rows 4 and 5).
- **A node.** The page is the fleet's and has no input. Each storage point's own 21 days are in a table no page draws by point; the site's node list is day-ahead (rows 4, 10 and 13).
- **A duration.** The model beside the awards is 2 hours, and the awards are not split by the batteries' hours: ERCOT's day-ahead file states none. The real-time file's state of charge would give a floor (row 12; part A, row 7).
- **The offers where the fleet trades.** The price bands are of day-ahead curves, which carried a third of the fleet's energy; the real-time offer curves are held raw and are in no table (row 11). And the gap's three parts are for seven months together, not for a month (row 9).
