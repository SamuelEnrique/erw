## What this reader still cannot get from the tool, and why

- **Gas this morning.** On the board of 16 November Henry Hub is 3 November's close, stale, and every spark spread stops on 6 November: the board's gas set has not been taken from the builder since (row 2). The warehouse holds 11 November. EIA posts its daily spot prices weekly, so even when fixed the newest gas close is days old; the regional hubs (Waha, Houston Ship Channel and the rest) are licensed indices.
- **New York, New England, PJM and MISO.** License needed or paused: a license or a person's review of the terms, not work (row 12).
- **Carbon beyond California.** RGGI and the EUA are licensed (row 13); California's allowance is quarterly, so "a year before" is the same auction a year before.
- **A complete ERCOT month and SPP's real time.** Two ERCOT day-ahead days and ten SPP real-time days of the last six weeks are missing (rows 7 and 11).
- **A futures curve.** CME Group's license (the note's decision 11).
