# The home battery game (/play/battery): the cross-check, 16 November 2026

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), session 7535 (chain 304, part A, the third part). The game's prices are real and its
rules are its own; what can stand against a published figure is the days it plays (the prices and the claims the day
list makes about them), the rules it borrows from the market (the USD 5,000 cap, the value of lost load) and the real
fleet it sets beside its own. Ours is what the page shows or rests on; theirs is the publisher's own figure. No figure
was adjusted to match.

**What was read, and how.** Robots files first: potomaceconomics.com (it disallows `/wp-admin/` only) and ercot.com
(`/services/comm/mkt_notices/` is not among its disallowed paths). ERCOT's terms of use (`/help/terms`, section 5): its
public content "may be used, reproduced, and redistributed, provided that the contents are not modified"; section 6
forbids use that affects the site's performance (one page read). Potomac Economics publishes no terms page that a search
of its site found; its reports are public PDFs, read once each. The two reports were saved to the session's scratch
folder (`runs/`, not in git) and read as text with pdfplumber; the page numbers below are the PDF's pages. Nothing was
sent but the request for each file; no contact string was needed. Not read: CAISO's own account of the two California
days, EIA's grid monitor, nerc.com.
