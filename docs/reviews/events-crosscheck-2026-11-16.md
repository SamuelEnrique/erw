# /events and its nine event pages: the cross-check, 16 November 2026

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), session 7531 (chain 304, part A), on samueloldlaptop. Each event's headline figures,
as `event_window_daily` holds them (the rows the pages print; retrieved 19 October 2026, copied beside the replication
in `replication/events/window_rows.csv`), beside the operator's, FERC's and NERC's own published account of the event.
No figure is adjusted to match. The method: [`docs/methods/events.md`](../methods/events.md).

**What was read, and how.** Each host's robots file first: ferc.gov, caiso.com, ercot.com, pjm.com, eia.gov and
insidelines.pjm.com allow the paths read (insidelines.pjm.com's robots file was read just after its one page, not
before: said under "Decisions made without you" in the session report). **nerc.com was not read:** its robots file
disallows named AI agents (ClaudeBot among them) from the whole site, so the FERC, NERC and Regional Entity reports are
read from FERC's own copies on ferc.gov. ERCOT's paths under `/news/release/` are not among its disallowed paths
(`/content/news` is). PJM's Winter Storm Elliott report (pjm.com, 17 July 2023) could not be read today (the file is
over the reader's 10 MB limit): the page's quotes of it are session 39's, and FERC's report gives PJM's own peak figure
(row 8). Every PDF was read as text with pdfplumber; nothing was sent but the request for the file.

**Why the two differ, in general.** The ERW's figures are EIA-930's hourly demand of each balancing authority, by the
grid's local day, as the operator reported it to EIA. An operator's own peak may be an instantaneous or five-minute
reading (CAISO), an hourly integrated load after settlement (ERCOT: "the integrated system load for the full hour"),
or a load with transmission losses allocated (PJM's figure in FERC's report). A difference within about 2 percent
is of that kind; none here is larger.
