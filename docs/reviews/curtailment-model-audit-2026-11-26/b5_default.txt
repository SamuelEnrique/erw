Decisions on this page (each with its default and its source on hover) Each grid's number is what that grid publishes: CAISO's and SPP's own curtailment, ERCOT's output below the High Sustained Limit as the ERW's estimate, and no figure for a grid that publishes none or needs a license; the three are not comparable and are never summed. (sessions 18 and 144) i Default: Each operator's own figure, labelled; never a sum across grids. Source: CAISO's production and curtailment data and Daily Renewable Report; SPP's curtailment reports; ERCOT's hourly wind and solar reports. ERCOT publishes no curtailment figure: output below the limit is max(0, limit less output) each hour, wind and solar each on its own, and includes ramping, telemetry and averaging, so the page calls it "output below HSL", never curtailment. (session 18) i Default: Called output below HSL; the ERW's estimate. Source: ERCOT, Hourly Averaged Actual and Forecasted Values (wind NP4-742-CD, solar by region); docs/methods/curtailment.md, "ERCOT". A share is curtailed MWh over curtailed plus wind and solar output, over the days of the month both are held, written when those days hold at least 95 percent of the month's hours; one output source a month, nothing scaled up to a whole month. (session 144) i Default: Over the days both are held; 95 percent of the month's hours. Source: docs/methods/curtailment.md, "The share of available output". SPP's share divides SPP's own curtailment by itself plus EIA-930's hourly wind and solar generation for SPP in the same hours: the operator's curtailment over a denominator the ERW builds from EIA's hours. (session 144) i Default: SPP's curtailment over EIA-930's SPP generation. Source: SPP's curtailment reports; EIA Form EIA-930, hourly generation by fuel for SWPP. A year's share is the same division over the months of the year that have a share, their curtailed MWh over their curtailed plus output MWh, never another denominator. (session 144) i Default: The months with a share, summed then divided. Source: docs/methods/curtailment.md, "The share of available output". California by the hour, by month and by reason counts a month when at least 90 percent of its days are held, its figure the sum over the days held, never scaled up; hours and months are Pacific. (session 98) i Default: 90 percent of the days; Pacific time. Source: CAISO's five-minute curtailment record and its daily report by hour from 2026; docs/methods/caiso_curtailment_intervals.md. (session 98) i Default: System totals by month; no claim of what could have been stored. Source: CAISO's Today's Outlook battery output; docs/methods/caiso_curtailment_intervals.md, "The batteries". Where free energy is counts the hours priced below zero and under USD 5 per MWh, real time where a place holds it for 95 percent of the window, else day-ahead, in each grid's standard time; a count needs 95 percent of the window's hours. (session 144) i Default: Below zero and under USD 5; real time where held, else day-ahead. Source: The operators' public hub and zone prices; docs/methods/curtailment.md, "Where free energy is". What curtailment is worth values each hour's curtailed MWh at the hub's price of that hour, a month written when the curtailment covers 90 percent of its days and the price 95 percent of its hours; an hour with no price is left out of both sides. (session 144) i Default: Each hour at the hub's price; 90 and 95 percent coverage. Source: docs/methods/curtailment.md, "What it is worth".
