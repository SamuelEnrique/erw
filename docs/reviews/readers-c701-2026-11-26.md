# Chain 701's new files read as their readers (part F, 26 November 2026)

Session 10000 (lab-desktop, data machine, first window). The files this chain built or rebuilt, read as five readers
would read them: the equities files (`site/data/equities/`, which window E's `/equities` page draws) and the large-load
files (`site/data/siting/large_load_rules.json`, the tab under `/cost-of-power?view=allgrids`, and the laws under
`/policy`). Each case is checked against the warehouse's own table or research file, and the pages were read on gate 6's own build (`af8a5a4eb`, served at localhost:3761 during its value checks, 16:26 UTC, unlocked as a reviewer): `/equities`, `/equities?p=henry_hub&win=5y`, `/equities?p=wti&win=1y`, `/cost-of-power?view=allgrids` (with a row opened), `/policy?view=monitor` (and Maryland), `/impact`. Every gap is a numbered row.

## The five cases

1. **An energy investor on a day oil falls.** WTI's last close in `energy_prices.json` (17 November 2026, USD 89.53)
   and its day, week and month moves (from 89.24 on 16 November, 89.62 on 10 November, 108.21 on 16 October) equal
   `eia_fuel_spot_prices` row for row; Brent and Henry Hub the same. EIA publishes these prices weekly, so on 26
   November the newest is nine days old; the page says so: its table gives each price's own date (WTI, Brent and Henry Hub
   "17 Nov 2026", ERCOT's and CAISO's day-ahead "25 Nov 2026"), and the day, week, month, year-to-date and year moves
   (WTI +0.32, -0.10, -17.26, +56.36, +47.59 percent) are the file's.
2. **A utility analyst comparing two utilities.** Georgia Power's large-load rule (in effect; threshold "an expected
   peak demand of 100 MW or greater ...", p. 16, its registry file) and Old Dominion Electric Cooperative's (in effect;
   threshold not published, with what was searched) read as the research file holds them; the page file resolves the
   registry reference to the value and its cite.
3. **A student of one company.** No company is held: SEC EDGAR refuses the ERW's contact string and no free share price
   may be kept, so `index.json` carries no company and says why in `held.companies` (gap R2, Samuel's ruling).
4. **A datacenter developer reading a utility's large-load rule.** Rhode Island Energy's extra-large load tariff
   (ordered 20 November 2026): threshold 20 MW with page and quote, the 300 MW application under study, each term the
   order does not set marked not published with what was searched. Evergy Kansas South's rule is held (internal) and
   appears as a held row with its utility and place only. On the page the rules table is drawn in the browser; the node
   render test draws it whole (124 of 124 rules shown, 238 blank cells that say license needed). The policy page lists
   Maryland's S.B. 937 and H.B. 1532 as in effect, "Signed, and its effective date has passed", with their dates.
5. **A trader looking for what moved with gas.** Henry Hub's day (3.20 to 3.10, -3.13 percent), week and month moves
   are there, and the hub power prices beside it; no equity moves (R2), and no figure pairs a price's move with gas's
   (gap R3).
