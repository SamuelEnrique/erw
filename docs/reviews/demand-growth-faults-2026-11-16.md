## What published figures stand against

The cross-check is the table "What published figures stand against" of `docs/methods/demand_growth.md`, ten rows. In short: the Lower 48's growth of 7.03 percent from 2019 to 2025 stands beside 7.05 percent for EIA's retail sales (Electric Power Monthly, table 5.1); fourteen highest hours (the seven grids' records and their 2026 summer peaks) equal what EIA published on 15 October 2026, to its rounding; PJM's and CAISO's own peaks fall on the same days and hours, within 1.5 percent; ERCOT's growth is 3 points above Texas's retail sales; California's two figures have opposite signs (row 12). The tool holds no forecast, so PJM's, NERC's, Grid Strategies' and the EIA's forecasts have nothing of ours beside them. Nothing published stands against a month's or an hour's growth, the hours used, or MISO's and SPP's average demand; ERCOT's own site was not asked.
