# The rulings that move a figure, ranked (17 November 2026)

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW). Written by chain 402 (sessions 7900 on, the old laptop) for Samuel's review of 17 November 2026. Each entry is one question that waits for his ruling and that changes a figure a reader sees by 1 percent or more, or adds or removes a figure. Nothing here is decided and nothing was changed: no page, no default, no branch landed. A recommendation is not a ruling.

## How to read it

- **Three sections.** First the questions that change a headline figure: a number the page puts first (its headline tiles, its first sentence, a calculator's result as it opens), in any view the page's own menu reaches (a grid, a state, a month). Then the questions that change another figure (a table cell, a figure that depends on what the reader types). Then the questions that add or remove a figure.
- **Order inside a section:** largest change first. The change is the largest percent the measurement gives for that question. A change in points of a share is ranked by its points. Where the other choice shows other figures and computes none differently (another year, another list), it is ranked as 100 percent. In the third section the order is by how many figures are added or removed.
- **Where each figure is from:** the measurement file of each tool (written on 17 November from a script's printout) and the record of the branch that built the other choice (its test pins the figure). Both are linked under each entry. A figure the two give differently is listed at the foot under "Figures that do not agree", and that entry carries no recommendation.
- **A recommendation** is keep (the page as it is), switch (the other choice) or "no basis to recommend". It rests only on a published figure, a ruling Samuel already made, his page standard, or the tool's own method note. Where none of these decides, it says what there is to weigh.
- **Screenshots** are taken by the desktop today, one of the other choice and one of the page as it is. An entry says "screenshot not taken yet" until its pictures are pushed.
- **To rule:** write after `Ruling:` under an entry. The questions that are not here are in [the defaults list](https://github.com/SamuelEnrique/erw/blob/main/docs/reviews/rulings-defaults-2026-11-17.md), which can be closed with one "defaults stand".

### 1. Local impact (the forecast tool), New York: New York: all sales tax exempt, or only the machinery's share

- **Page:** Local impact (the forecast tool), New York, https://erw-flame.vercel.app/impact
- **Question:** For a plant in New York the tool treats all of the sales tax as exempt. New York's exemption is for generating machinery, and in the other states with a machinery exemption the tool exempts only the machinery's share of the purchases. Which should New York use?
- **Now:** 100 percent exempt: sales and use tax USD 0 for each of New York's default plants. "Taxes, present value": wind (200 MW) USD 4.17 million, solar (150 MW) USD 7.08 million, gas (1,227 MW) USD 23.55 million.
- **Other choice:** Wind 66.9 percent exempt, solar 67.2, gas 58.3, as the other states hold it. Sales and use tax USD 8.66 million (wind), 4.81 million (solar), 29.56 million (gas). "Taxes, present value": wind USD 12.83 million, solar 11.89 million, gas 52.14 million.
- **Change:** "Taxes, present value": wind +208 percent, solar +68 percent, gas +121 percent. Sales and use tax appears where USD 0 stood.
- **Branch:** [wip/alt-impact-2](https://github.com/SamuelEnrique/erw/tree/wip/alt-impact-2)
- **Screenshots:** screenshot not taken yet
- **Recommendation:** **switch**. The tool's method note states its own rule: a machinery exemption "exempts the generating machinery and not the construction materials", held as the machinery's share of purchases. New York's statute (Tax Law 1115(a)(12)) is such an exemption, and the page's own hover calls the 100 percent "an upper bound of the exemption".
