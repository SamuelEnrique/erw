- **What the rule is now, after the first hand check**: a story's name must be whole words of the filed name (never the other way round: the first rule matched "Monomoy Capital" to a filer called "Capital Energy"), at least four characters, the corporate words dropped (LLC, Inc, Corp, Co, Company, LP, Ltd, PLC, Holdings; ENERGY is kept, since dropping it made "Energy Transfer" into "Transfer"). The first rule found 58 deals with a match and 9 in the quarter; the final rule 52 and 6, with the false "Capital" kind gone.
- **What would raise precision**: a party's own key in `energy_companies` (the Thesis Builder's company row, with its EQR filer names as aliases, as `ferc_eqr_buyer_names` groups the filers' own spellings), so that "Blackstone" the investor and "Blackstone Wind Farm" are two keys; and the deal's quarter in the EQR once Q3 is filed (late October) and loaded by the data machine.

## The rule

- **Keys.** Upper case; punctuation to spaces; the corporate words dropped unless nothing would be left; spaces folded. The filed buyer's name is first read through `ferc_eqr_buyer_names` (name to `x_key`, the group's key after session 99's rules) where it is listed.
- **A match.** The party's key equals the filed key, or is whole words of it and at least four characters long. A row matches a deal when one party matches the seller and another the buyer (either way round); a deal with one named party matches on that party as seller or buyer, said so ("one party").
- **The quarter.** The deal's date's quarter against the row's `x_quarter`; "any quarter" is every quarter the warehouse holds (one today).
