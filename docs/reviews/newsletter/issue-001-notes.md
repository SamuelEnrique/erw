# Issue 1, the notes: every claim, its source, and the sceptic's reply

Session 406, 21 October 2026. For [`issue-001.md`](issue-001.md). One line a claim, in the order the draft makes them. "Figure" claims are a number the page shows, with its check-values key (the footnote in the draft). "Arithmetic" claims are computed here from those figures and are not on any page. "Reading" claims are the draft's interpretation; the warehouse does not prove them. "Outside" claims rest on something the warehouse does not hold.

How each figure was read tonight: see "How the figures were read" at the end.

## Why this question

- **The question was chosen** from the four the chain named (the price years, the node basis, the 4CP saving, the battery's income by stream). The battery's income by stream is the one where the warehouse gives something a reader cannot easily get free elsewhere: one battery, every year since 2018, split by stream and by product, with its code and prices public. The sceptic: commercial services publish ERCOT battery revenue indices (Modo Energy's is the best known) and some of their headline numbers are public; they measure what real batteries earned, which this model does not. The draft says "usually sold, or given as one fleet average with no split", not "nowhere else": Samuel should check that sentence against what he knows of those services before it goes out.
