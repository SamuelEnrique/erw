## What a trader asks that the table cannot answer

1. **At what price.** No deal of the 492 states a price in US dollars per MWh. The news the ERW reads rarely prints
   one; FERC's Electric Quarterly Reports do, for the sellers that file them (`/contracts`, `ferc_eqr_contracts`,
   internal while its license stands).
2. **Where the power is delivered.** The table holds a state on 42 rows and no node, hub or zone, so a deal cannot be
   priced against the grid it sells into; the tracker reads a grid from the state, for the states one operator
   mostly serves.
3. **For how long, and in what shape.** A term on 17 rows; no shape (as generated, baseload, a block), no start date.
4. **How large.** A size in MW on 23 rows, in MWh on 2.
5. **Whether it closed.** The status is the story's on the day of the story; no later story is read for a change.

Each needs a pull the chain has not approved (a filing, a press release, a regulator's docket), not a change of the
page. The fields a later extraction could ask of the same stories (a delivery point, a start date, a shape) are named
here for the data machine; none is extracted tonight.
