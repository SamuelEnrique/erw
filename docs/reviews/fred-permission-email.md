Why it is needed: FRED's terms forbid, without the Bank's written consent, storing or archiving FRED content or putting it in a database. The sentence (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/legal/, read 2026-10-12 at 09:53 UTC): "You may not, without the Bank's prior written consent: [...] Store, cache, or archive any portion of the FRED® Services or FRED® Content; provide any stored, cached, or archived portion of the FRED® Services or FRED® Content to any third party; or incorporate any FRED® Content in any database, compilation, archive, cache, or other medium. [...] These series may be under copyright or in the public domain and may be used without permission, provided you do not engage in any prohibited use."
