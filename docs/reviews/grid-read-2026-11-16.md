## The reader and the five cases

The reader: **a utility's market analyst writing the Monday note on ERCOT**, who wants the latest hour, the mix, what ERCOT took from its neighbours, the year's records, and what the batteries did, each with its hour and source.

1. **The latest hour:** demand, and against the same hour a week earlier.
2. **The mix in that hour:** the shares of gas and wind, and whether the shares add up to the MW.
3. **The neighbours:** ERCOT's net import, from whom, and how old the figure is.
4. **The records of 2026:** ERCOT's highest hour of demand, and the solar and wind records.
5. **The batteries on 12 November 2026:** discharged, charged, the hour of most discharge.
