# Local impact (/impact) read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part B, session 7515, 16 November 2026)

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW). Branch `wip/7506-impact`. The owner reviews this page himself: its 23 faults of 13 November (`docs/reviews/forecast-faults-2026-11-13.md`) and session 7506's 12 rows (`docs/reviews/impact-faults-2026-11-16.md`) are not read again, and nothing the page draws was changed but one figure (row 1).

**The reader.** A state economic development office's analyst weighing an abatement (in Nevada, the Office of Energy's staff who prepare the Director's order). Why: every figure the page holds is a state's (JEDI's multipliers are statewide, each tax is the state's statute, the present value is at OMB's rate for a public benefit-cost analysis), and its A and B with the local option on and off is an abatement granted and refused; a county's own share, which a permitting lead needs for a commission hearing, is nowhere on it.

**The five cases.** (1) Lone Mountain Geothermal, Esmeralda County, Nevada, 36 MW, USD 178,446,043, order of 4 November 2026: what does the 55 percent abatement cost, year by year (a state with a property tax abatement). (2) Grant Solar, McCook County, South Dakota, 99 MW, planned 2027: what does the plant pay with no income tax and a statute's tax in place of property tax. (3) Silver Queen Wind Farm, Crawford County, Iowa, 252 MW, planned 2028: what does the county's special valuation give up (a state where the local levy is not held). (4) CPV Basin Ranch Energy Center, Ward County, Texas, 1,445 MW of combined cycle, planned 2029: what does the plant bring with its gas produced in Texas (a gas plant with fuel). (5) Cape Generating Station, Beaver County, Utah, 86 MW of geothermal, planned 2027, against the same plant in Nevada (A and B differ only by state).

**How it was read.** This machine cannot build the site. The page's own view (`site/app/impact/Forecast.tsx`) was drawn to HTML with the site's code for eight addresses, each with A and then B in view, and written as text (`site/scripts/impact-read.mjs`): every field of the left box with its default and hover, every cell and hover of the A and B table, the table by year, each chart column's hover words, the run's CSV. A click was read as the address the page would write after it. Not seen: a click, a chart, a hover as a mouse meets it, the layout at 390 px, the Python and Stata downloads, the planned-project list beyond the ten projects of `replication/impact/projects.csv`. No publisher was asked for anything: the only published claim set beside the page is Nevada's (the order and fiscal notes session 7506 read, `docs/methods/forecast.md`, section 12.3); for cases 2 to 5 no developer's or state's claim was read, and what is judged is whether the page's figures are in the units such a claim comes in.

## What this reader still cannot get from the tool, and why

- **The county's share.** Every job figure is the state's (JEDI holds no county), and no tax figure says who receives it. The county's levy is the reader's to type in every state but the five that publish a statewide figure, and the state's part of the sales tax, which the page's own file holds, is not drawn (row 3).
- **The figure a hearing compares.** A developer's or a state's claim counts workers at the site; the A and B table counts the three effects together, and the on-site figure is a hover and a CSV away (row 2). Against Nevada's order the page's on-site job-years stand 1.23 times the order's jobs, and its operating jobs stand against the applicant's zero.
- **What an abatement costs, in one figure.** A and B draw the abatement granted and refused, but not their difference and not what differs between them (rows 4 and 5); for a negotiated abatement (Texas chapter 312, a payment in lieu) there is no rule to switch at all, only the reader's own rate.
- **A sales tax after exemptions, and any sales tax for geothermal.** Where a state exempts generating equipment and the share is not held, the figure is an upper bound said two clicks from the figure (row 9); for geothermal no taxable share is held, so the cell is "not held" and the present value of taxes leaves it out (session 7506's rows 5 and 7).
- **A project that is not in EIA's inventory, and the claim itself.** Lone Mountain Geothermal has a state order of 4 November 2026 and is not among EIA-860M's planned generators of September 2026 (0 rows in `eia860m_planned_generators`), so it is typed, not picked. And the page holds no published claim: the cross-checks against 24 documents are in an internal note, so the reader brings the claim and sets it beside the page by hand.
