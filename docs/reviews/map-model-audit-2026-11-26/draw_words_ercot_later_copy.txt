Near a pointiType a point as latitude, longitude in decimal degrees (for example 32.4487, -99.7331, the form a web map copies), or press "Measure from this row" on a row's card; then a distance in miles. Both are needed. The distance is the great-circle distance on a sphere of the Earth's mean radius, 3,958.8 miles, from each row's own point: EIA's coordinates for a unit, the county's or the town's point for a queue position, the city's point for a datacenter, not their sites. Both ends are counted in; a row with no point is left out. The list under the map gives every row within it, nearest first.

Lines on the mapiTransmission lines of 230 kV and above are HIFLD's Electric Power Transmission Lines, and the HGL pipelines are EIA's layer of January 2020, both as Penn State's PASDA serves them; each is simplified for the web, and its hover names its table. EIA's layers of natural gas, crude oil and petroleum product pipelines, LNG terminals, refineries and underground gas storage are served from a host whose robots file refuses this reader, so they are not drawn; HIFLD's compressor stations and substations are not in a public service the ERW reads.
