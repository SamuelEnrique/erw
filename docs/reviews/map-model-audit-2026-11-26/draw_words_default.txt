Near a pointiType a point as latitude, longitude in decimal degrees (for example 32.4487, -99.7331, the form a web map copies), or press "Measure from this row" on a row's card; then a distance in miles. Both are needed. The distance is the great-circle distance on a sphere of the Earth's mean radius, 3,958.8 miles, from each row's own point, not its site: EIA's coordinates of the unit's plant for a unit, the county's, city's or town's point for a queue position, and for a datacenter the operator's own coordinates, its city's or town's point or its county's point (the list's "Placed at" says which). Both ends are counted in; a row with no point is left out. The list under the map gives every row within it, nearest first.

Lines on the mapiTransmission lines of 230 kV and above are HIFLD's Electric Power Transmission Lines, and the HGL pipelines are EIA's layer of January 2020, both as Penn State's PASDA serves them; each is simplified for the web, and its hover names its table. EIA's layers of natural gas, crude oil and petroleum product pipelines, LNG terminals, refineries and underground gas storage are served from a host whose robots file refuses this reader, so they are not drawn; HIFLD's compressor stations and substations are not in a public service the ERW reads. Every line HIFLD lists at 230 kV or more is drawn alike, whatever its status (in service, under construction, or a status HIFLD does not state) and whether HIFLD inferred its voltage; the date in a line's hover is the day the ERW read PASDA's copy, not the date of HIFLD's data, which the table keeps line by line. The method note says each.

Decisions on this page (12): each choice that moves a figure, with its default and its source
A unit's megawatts are its nameplate capacity. Net summer capacity, the measure of EIA's own published totals, is lower.iDefault: Nameplate capacity.Source: EIA, Form EIA-860M, "Nameplate Capacity (MW)". Over the operating units of the inventory of 2026-09, net summer capacity is 7.5 percent lower (docs/methods/map.md, section 12).Made in: session 105.
Megawatts are never added across kinds: a unit's nameplate, a queue position's request and a datacenter's stated load are three different things.iDefault: Each kind has its own count and its own sum.Source: A queue position is a request, and most are withdrawn before they are built (docs/methods/energy_projects.md); a datacenter's megawatts are a load a story or its operator states.Made in: session 167.
A queue position is on the map while it is not withdrawn: active, suspended, completed, or with no status stated. A completed position may be a unit the inventory already lists.iDefault: Withdrawn positions are left out.Source: The table energy_projects, its standard status; the first version of the map (session 16).Made in: sessions 16 and 167.
The queue positions drawn are ERCOT's, SPP's, CAISO's and ISO-NE's. MISO's and NYISO's are held back, and no queue of PJM is held.iDefault: Four queues.Source: Each grid operator's own terms: MISO's pulls are paused while its terms are reviewed (docs/methods/miso_pause.md); NYISO's legal notice confers no license.Made in: sessions 159 and 167.
A unit's grid is EIA's balancing authority for it. The seven ISOs are named; every other balancing authority, and a unit with none, is "Outside the seven ISOs".iDefault: EIA's balancing authority code.Source: EIA, Form EIA-860M, "Balancing Authority Code". In the inventory of 2026-09, 973 units have none: EIA leaves 727 blank and writes "NA" for 246.Made in: session 105.
A battery is a storage unit whose prime mover is a battery; pumped storage and the other kinds are "Other storage". A queue's storage request does not say what kind it is.iDefault: EIA prime mover code BA.Source: EIA, Form EIA-860 instructions, the prime mover codes.Made in: session 105.
A planned unit keeps EIA's own seven status codes, from approvals not started to construction complete; they are not folded into "planned" and "under construction".iDefault: EIA's code, in plain words.Source: EIA, Form EIA-860 instructions, the proposed status codes P, L, T, U, V, TS and OT; the owner's words of 8 October 2026.Made in: session 167.
A queue position is drawn at its county's point, or its city's or town's, not at its site. A name the Census Bureau's files do not hold in its state leaves the position off the drawing.iDefault: The internal point of the county, the place or the town; no fuzzy matching.Source: U.S. Census Bureau, 2025 Gazetteer Files (counties, places, county subdivisions); docs/methods/energy_projects.md, "Coordinates".Made in: sessions 16 and 349.
When a size is asked for, a row whose source states no megawatts is left out: it cannot be said to fit one. Both ends of the size are counted in.iDefault: No size: every row.Source: A choice of session 167, stated in the method note; no source sets it.Made in: session 167.
A distance from a point is measured from each row's own point: EIA's coordinates of the unit's plant for a unit, the county's, city's or town's point for a queue position, and for a datacenter the operator's own coordinates, its city's or town's point or its county's point (the list's "Placed at" says which). Once a distance is asked for, a row with no point is left out.iDefault: No point: every row. Both ends of the distance are counted in.Source: The great-circle distance (the haversine formula) on a sphere of the Earth's mean radius, 3,958.8 miles; the read of 16 November 2026 (docs/reviews/map-read-2026-11-16.md, row 1).Made in: session 7537.
A datacenter is drawn when the tracker places it in a US state. A facility abroad, or one no story names a state for, is counted in the file and not drawn.iDefault: The facilities with a US state.Source: The table datacenter_facilities, its state column (docs/methods/datacenter_facilities.md).Made in: session 167.
Retired units are not on the map, and nothing since the inventory's month is in its units (the queue positions and the datacenters are of their own, later days, in the source line).iDefault: Operating and planned units of one month.Source: EIA, Form EIA-860M, published about a month after the month it describes; retired units are in the table eia860m_retired_generators.Made in: session 105.
