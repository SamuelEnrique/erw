# /contracts read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part B, session 7510, 16 November 2026)

**The reader:** a credit analyst at a fund that lends against contracted power plants, checking the price, the term and the counterparty of one offtake contract against what sellers filed with FERC. Why this reader and not a developer or a trader: the page was built for a credit investor's question (session 83), its columns are an underwriter's (seller, buyer, affiliate, term, price as filed), it holds no technology for a developer and no delivered volume for a trader.
**The five cases:** (1) a unit power energy contract of 15 years delivered in SPP, signed in 2025 Q3: its price in USD per MWh against the long-term energy contracts signed for delivery in SPP in 2025; (2) a resource adequacy capacity contract delivered in CAISO, signed between 2025 Q3 and 2026 Q2: its price in USD per kW-month against the capacity contracts signed there in those four quarters; (3) what a buyer in the Southeast (the Carolinas, Georgia, Florida) pays for capacity under contracts signed since January 2024; (4) a tolling agreement for a battery in California signed in 2024 or 2025: the toll against the other tolling contracts filed; (5) the buyer of case 1's contract, a utility in SPP: how many energy contracts in force it holds and how many megawatts they state.
**How it was read:** this machine cannot build the site. The page's own default export was drawn to HTML for 33 addresses (each case's quarters, products and balancing authorities, the three other views by product, and four odd addresses) on the rows of this machine (the load of 5 October 2026: 18,989 rows), and every cell and every hover was read as text; the counts below were taken again from the rows the page is served. Then the page was drawn again after the fixes. Not seen: a click, a hover opening, the layout at any width (the page has no chart). The live database was not read. The table is internal: no party's name, no row and no party's own figure is written here, only counts and the page's own words.

## Each case, start to finish

- **1. SPP, energy, 2025.** `/contracts?q=2025-Q3&product=energy&ba=SWPP`, then the other three quarters. The sentence gives the rows, agreements, sellers and buyers of each quarter; the reader adds four quarters by hand (14 rows). Every number is in $/MWH, so the nine can be set side by side. Eight terms are drawn in years, six rows file no end. No row states a quantity: a contract of a given size has nothing to be matched to. The technology is the seller's name, as the page's first limit says. Rows 1 to 5, 8, 9, 12, 13, 21.
- **2. CAISO, capacity.** `/contracts?q=2025-Q3&product=capacity&ba=CISO` and three more. The buyer's cell marks the 15 rows between affiliates. The prices come in three units and four are 0. Most terms are a month or a quarter. Twelve rows of one agreement carry twelve prices on the same dates. Rows 1, 2, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12, 16, 18, 19.
- **3. The Southeast, capacity.** The reader has to know that the Carolinas are "CPLE" and "DUK" and Georgia "SOCO", and to open each quarter to find the three that hold a row. 850 of the 1,440 capacity rows listed name no balancing authority, so a place's rows are the ones that say so, not all there are. Rows 1 to 3, 5, 7, 8, 12, 13, 14.
- **4. Tolling.** `/contracts?q=2024-Q2&product=tolling` and five more quarters; `/contracts?view=terms`; the two rankings for tolling. In the standing book 216 tolling contracts are in force and 3 state megawatts (524.9 MW); every "MW filed" cell of the 25 largest buyers reads "none filed". Storage is tagged on no row. Rows 2, 5, 11, 20, 22.
- **5. The buyer's book.** `/contracts?view=largest&product=energy` and `/contracts?view=mw&product=energy`: 22,808 energy contracts in force between 2,824 sellers and 4,517 buyers; 356 state megawatts (31,402.3 MW). A buyer in the first 25 is read in one line; any other is not on the page. No row of the list links to its buyer's line. Rows 10, 15, and part A's rows 7 and 8.
