## How it was read, and what could not be

- **What the built site shows, to every reader:** every one of the thirteen addresses prints the hold, "license needed. The hold covers every row of the table. All 529 rows of energy_deals are held from 2026-10-21", and nothing of a row: no table, no chart, no download (the download line reads "energy_deals held: license needed"). The internal cookie opens the page in review but does not lift a license hold (`site/lib/license.ts`: the hold reaches every read through `lib/supabase.ts`, and no environment variable turns it off). A local lift of the hold for the read was refused by the session's permission layer as a weakening of a control, and was not tried another way.
- **So the rows were read through the pages' own functions**, run in Node on the live set's rows (the same select as `lib/data.ts` `deals()`, 529 rows, the anon key): `fold`, `toDeal`, `comparables`, `priceCells`, `aiCells`, `largest`, `mwByQuarter`, `aiShare`, `topParties` of `site/lib/deals2.ts` and `dealsai.ts`, and `rowOf`, `filterRows`, `sortRows`, `toCsv` of `dealsview.ts` (`runs/s1851/recompute-deals.mjs`, output `runs/s1851/recompute.json`, the CSV as the page's download would write it in `runs/s1851/view.csv`). Every sort, every filter and the CSV were exercised on those functions; the hovers, the open row and the clicks were read from the components' code (`DealsTable.tsx`, the views), since the browser had nothing to click.
- **The medians recomputed by hand** (my own Python on the live rows, `runs/s1851/` session log): power purchase agreements, 9 deals state MW, median 509 (the page's functions: 9, 509); ERCOT, 3 state MW, 50, 222 and 1,200, median 222 (page: 3, 222); solar, 3 state MW, 135, 509 and 2,100, median 509 (page: 3, 509). They agree. The year 2026 differs by the folded pairs (29 against 28 and 509 against 564.5 before folding), as the method says it should.
- **The twenty largest AI-power rows** (ten by MW, ten by US dollars) were read against their stories' titles and summaries (`news_stories.csv` of 5 November, the same day as the live rows) and the evidence sentences (`energy_deals_evidence.csv`). Every MW and every dollar figure is in its headline. What is wrong is in rows 9 to 12 below.
- **At 390 px** no page scrolls sideways (scroll width 390 of 390 on all thirteen). At 1280 the tracker and the views draw at 1265.
