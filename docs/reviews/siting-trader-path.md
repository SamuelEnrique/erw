## The questions

A power trader opens the tab to find where a load's energy is cheap and how it moves. The chain prompt names the path (session 723's section); each question below is one of its clauses.

1. **Where is a load's energy cheapest, grid by grid, day-ahead and real time, and how has it moved over the year?**
2. **Inside ERCOT: which hubs and zones are cheapest, and by how much do they differ?**
3. **ERCOT's nodes sorted by energy: which nodes are cheapest, and what is each one's basis to the hub?**
4. **ERCOT's nodes sorted by basis: where is the basis widest (positive) and where most negative?**
5. **A node's panel: how is its basis distributed over the year, month by month?**
6. **Two nodes as A and B: the difference per MWh and a year.**
7. **The hub against its nodes: what does a node pay over or under its hub?**
8. **The hours a flexible load gives up, and what they save.**
9. **The four-peak charge: firm against off, at the Commission's rate, per MWh for this load.**
10. **Can the screen be sent?**

## What broke, or was not held, each with its cause

Fixed by nothing in this session (the chain's rule: 723 fixes nothing; 724 does).

1. **A node cannot be chosen as A or B in the box** (step 9). `AllGridsView` hands `AllGridsForm` the hub rows only (`rows={hubs.map(...)}`), so the two selects list 48 options; `rowOf` in `lib/siting.ts` resolves an `ercot:` node from the address, so the headline and the panel draw for a node, but the selects read "none" and the summary "A and B set". **Inside this chain's files.**
2. **The headline and the A and B panel are not drawn in the node view** (step 9): `x.rows === "hubs" ? <Headline/><AbPanel/>` in `AllGridsView.tsx`. A trader comparing two nodes sees them above the hub table, not beside the node list. **Inside this chain's files.**
3. **The node panel's "Close" drops the screen** (step 8): its link is `?view=allgrids&rows=nodes`, not the current address less `node`. **Inside this chain's files.**
4. **The status line lowercases the hub's name** (step 7): `HEADS[by].toLowerCase()` in `AllGridsNodes.tsx` turns "Basis to HB_HUBAVG" into "basis to hb_hubavg". **Inside this chain's files.**
5. **The basis spread cannot be sorted by** (steps 6, 8): the node file holds each node's 10th, 50th and 90th percentile (`n.pct`, drawn in the panel), and the list has no column for the spread. A column "spread, p10 to p90" would be a new keyed figure from the same file, moving none. **Inside this chain's files; it adds a key.**
6. **Sorting by transmission under a profile that is off at the peaks sorts a tie** (step 13), and the sort line does not say the sort reads the profile's own figure (not the firm one). A line of words in the sort status; no figure moves. **Inside this chain's files.**
7. **The shared line's tips name a different source from the column's** (step 4): the months to connect in the shared line cite `lbnl_interconnection_queue` ("From queue entry to operation, 227 requests online from 2023 to 2025"), the column's tip cites `interconnection_durations` (session 718's table, from Berkeley Lab's file). The figure is the same; the words differ. **Inside this chain's files.**
8. **A node's own hub and zone are not held** (steps 4, 7, 10): the basis is to HB_HUBAVG only, so a node cannot be read against its own hub. The gaps file, rows 9 and 10 (ERCOT's settlement point mapping and resource registration); chain 062's session 733. **A gap, not a fix.**
9. **A node's price in the four peak intervals is not held** (step 8): `node_basis_daily` begins 1 October 2025 and holds days. The gaps file, row 13. **A gap.**
10. **The four peaks of the energy window are not published** (steps 11, 12): summer 2026's four intervals come by 1 December 2026, so a flexible load's hours off are the strike's alone, and the charge is computed on summer 2025's. The tip says so. The gaps file, row 7. **A gap, which ends by itself.**
11. **Real time is not held at the nodes** (step 5): the energy tip of a node says "Real time is not held at the nodes for twelve months"; `node_basis_daily` holds the day-ahead basis only (`dam_basis`). **A gap: the warehouse's table would need a real-time basis variable.** Not in the gaps file yet; 724 adds the row.
12. **Houston's utility is a small cooperative** (step 13): HB_HOUSTON's utility column reads "San Bernard Electric Coop, Inc and 2 more", USD 89.39 per MWh, because Harris County's three territories are CenterPoint (no sales row: a wires utility), Entergy Texas (MISO) and San Bernard. Session 718's ruling item (the open-items table, line 25) and gaps row 17. **For the ruling; not changed.**

## What a trader would ask that the tab does not answer

Listed, not built (the chain adds no page and no model).

- **A forward view**: every figure is the last twelve months; no forward curve, no futures settle (the board's curve column says "licensed source needed").
- **The basis by hour of day and by month as a sortable figure**, not only the mean and the three percentiles.
- **Real-time basis at the nodes**, and the day-ahead to real-time spread by node.
- **A node's zone and hub**, so a node is read against the hub a trader hedges at (item 8).
- **A search box over the 873 nodes** by name or class: the list sorts, and does not filter.
- **The 15-minute prices of the four peak intervals at a node** (item 9).
