== hovers (count, words)
1x The mean of the on-peak means of the latest 7 weekdays that hold one (hours ending 7 to 22 local, Monday to Friday, NERC holidays off), so the 7 can run over 9 calendar days. Monday to Friday in every grid, CAISO included, where the group "Power, on-peak and off-peak" takes Monday to Saturday.
1x The mean of the off-peak means of the latest 7 days: every hour outside the on-peak block, the whole day on weekends and NERC holidays. CAISO's Saturdays are off-peak here and on-peak in the group "Power, on-peak and off-peak".
1x Real time less day-ahead: the mean of the week's 7 daily differences, the real-time daily mean less the day-ahead daily mean. The group "Day-ahead minus real time, by hub" takes the other order, so its 7-day avg is this figure with its sign turned.
1x The week's hours in which the hour's real-time price ran more than 50 USD/MWh above its day-ahead price. A 15-minute market's hour is the mean of its four 15-minute prices, so a 15-minute spike the hour's mean hides is not counted.
1x The sample standard deviation of the 30 day-over-day changes in the day-ahead daily mean, over the 31 days to the date shown, in USD/MWh: not annualized and not a percent (prices can be zero or negative, so no log returns are taken). It needs all 31 days, so one missing day leaves the last one standing, marked stale, for the 31 days after.
11x the latest 7 weekdays with an on-peak mean, to 25 Nov 2026; the change is on the 7 such weekdays before
6x SPP's real-time prices are held (the real-time rows of the groups above), but the week's real-time measures are not computed for SPP: the trader view does not read them.
1x ISO-NE's own terms ask for permission to republish its prices, and none is recorded yet (docs/licenses.md, site/data/license_status.json): license needed. This row is held, not shown, until a permission is recorded.
1x NYISO's own terms ask for permission to republish its prices, and none is recorded yet (docs/licenses.md, site/data/license_status.json): license needed. This row is held, not shown, until a permission is recorded.
1x MISO's prices are paused while its terms are reviewed; no MISO value is shown.
1x PJM publishes its prices under a license the ERW does not hold.
