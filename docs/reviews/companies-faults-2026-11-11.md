- **Every footprint figure clicked through to its rows:** 2,732 keyed figures were read off the 34 addresses at 1280 (each `data-n` with its `data-raw`, its text and its hover). The view's drawing of the rows (rows 13 to 16) was read from `site/app/deals/capital/page.tsx` and `GroupedBars.tsx`, not from a page.
- **At 390 px** no address scrolls sideways (390 of 390 on all 34); at 1280 they draw at 1265. Stata is not on this machine: the do-files were read, not run.
