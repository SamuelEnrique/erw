The tour 16 stops through the Energy Research Warehouse for a first visit, about 10 minutes in all: the three tools, the grid, research, the price board and the daily digest, then the whole country: its regions, a storm across it, demand, trade and the resource map. Each stop says what to look at and opens the page. 16 stops 1. What a battery earns about 45 seconds A grid battery of your size in ERCOT or CAISO: what it earned from selling power and from the grid's reserves, year by year, and whether that pays for building it. 2. What a generator earns about 45 seconds The other side of every price: what a solar farm, a wind farm, a battery or a gas peaker earns at a grid's main hub, month by month. 3. What a datacenter pays about 45 seconds A large load of your size on a grid: what its power will cost, whether the power will be there, how clean it is and how soon it can connect. 4. The grid about 40 seconds One grid at a time, ERCOT first: what it is doing now, where its power comes from, who runs it and what it costs. 5. Research about 40 seconds Ask for an analysis of the warehouse's data, and read today's chart, this week's and the library of findings, each with its data and its code. 6. The price board about 30 seconds Every price the warehouse holds on one screen: power by hub and zone, gas, oil and refined products, with the day's moves. 7. The Energy Digest about 30 seconds The weekday brief: the day's energy news, scored and ranked, with the day's numbers from the warehouse and a chart. 8. Every region of the grid about 40 seconds The Grid tool beyond the seven grids: the thirteen regions EIA reports, six of them new to the site, each with its mix, its trade and the balancing authorities inside it, and the map of every balancing authority under the network view. 9. A storm across the country about 40 seconds An event page now follows the storm beyond one grid: each region across the event, what ran against the baseline, and the power that moved between regions. 10. Demand in every region about 35 seconds How much power each place uses, year by year since 2019, for the grids and the regions and balancing authorities of the whole country, two places side by side. 11. Who trades with whom about 35 seconds Supply and trade for the whole country: each region's net imports by month and by neighbour, and the pairs that trade, with the gap where the two sides' reports differ. 12. What is under the ground and in the wind about 35 seconds The resource map: where the coal, the gas, the sun and the wind are, each layer in its own unit, beside what is built or planned. A resource is not a power plant. 13. Where energy goes about 35 seconds Energy by industry, by fuel and by state: who uses the country's energy, as the publishers count it. 14. Energy per AI query about 30 seconds The energy a query to an AI model uses, as each publisher printed it, with who measured it and how. 15. Every state about 35 seconds One state, or two side by side: what it runs on and how that changed since 2001, what its homes, businesses and industry pay, what is built and coming, and who operates its grid. 16. Grid conditions about 35 seconds Is a grid tight, and is it congested: demand against the forecast, the megawatts out of service, the constraints binding and the alerts in force, on one time axis, for any day or for one of the nine events.
