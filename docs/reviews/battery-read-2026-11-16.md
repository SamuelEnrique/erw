# What a battery earns read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part B, session 7519, finished by session 7522, 16 November 2026)

**The reader:** a merchant battery developer choosing between two ERCOT nodes for a 100 to 200 MW project, who will also hear a buyer offer a toll. Chosen because the page's questions are a merchant developer's (revenue by stream, the worst month, debt coverage, the toll at which coverage reaches 1.25 times, NPV and IRR) and the chain file names this reader.
**Five cases:** (1) a 100 MW, 2-hour battery at ERCOT's West hub (`HB_WEST`) against one at the Houston hub (`HB_HOUSTON`), the day-ahead schedule, October 2025 to September 2026; (2) the same battery at 2 hours against 4 hours, under both strategies, and its years from 2018; (3) a 200 MW, 4-hour battery in ERCOT against the same in CAISO; (4) the 100 MW, 4-hour battery offered a toll on its whole power at USD 12.08 per kW-month (Lazard's levelized cost of storage at its low end, the cross-check row "Scenario A, breakeven toll") against staying merchant; (5) the same battery if a storm like Winter Storm Uri (February 2021) came again once in its life.
**How it was read:** Energy Research Warehouse (ERW), page `/cost-of-power/battery`, in review. This machine cannot build the site. The page's view was drawn to HTML by its own components (`node --import ./scripts/tsx-register.mjs`) on the warehouse's `battery_stack_monthly` (rows retrieved 21 October 2026, 2018-01 to 2026-09) and `battery_stack_stress_daily` (rows retrieved 2 October 2026), ten addresses, every cell and hover read as text (`runs/7519/c1_*` to `c5_*`, not committed). The server part of the page (`page.tsx`) was read as source. No click was made and no chart was seen. Session 7519 found and fixed the rows below; it was stopped before it wrote this file, and session 7522 wrote it from its commits, its test, its case files and the note's section 11.4.

Part A's work on this page is the done line of earlier chains (`docs/methods/battery_earns_algorithm.md`, `replication/battery/`); this file holds the read only.
