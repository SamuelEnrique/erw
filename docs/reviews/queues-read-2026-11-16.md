# The interconnection queue (`/queues`) read as the reader it is for (chain 304, part C, session 7545, 16 November 2026)

**The reader:** a developer deciding which grid's queue to file in: a merchant storage developer first, then a solar-with-storage, a wind and an offshore wind developer, and the energy lead of a large load (a data center) who reads the same page for the time to connect. Every figure is a row of Berkeley Lab and GridTracker's Queued Up, 2026 edition (one row a request, through the end of 2025; CC BY 4.0), summarized in `interconnection_queue_summary` and read from the site's copy `site/data/queues.json` (built 4 October 2026).
**The five cases:** (1) a 200 MW standalone battery, ERCOT against CAISO: how much storage is waiting, what share of the requests of 2000 to 2020 was built or withdrawn, how long the built ones took; (2) a 250 MW solar-with-storage project, ERCOT against PJM, on the same three questions; (3) an SPP wind developer: what became of the requests that entered in 2018, set beside the operators' own count on `/explore/interconnection`; (4) offshore wind in NYISO and ISO-NE (whose operators' own queue tables are held for a license); (5) a 500 MW data center in ERCOT, filing in 2026: what the page says of a load's wait.
**How it was read:** this machine cannot build the site. The page's own function (`site/app/queues/page.tsx`) was drawn to HTML for 16 addresses (`site/scripts/queues-page.mjs`, new), every cell, bar hover and tip read as text; the other pages that share a figure were read from their own files and code. Not seen: no click, no chart, no hover in a browser, no layout at 390 px.

The tool's done line (lab desktop, chain 301): `docs/methods/interconnection_queue_summary.md` (its replication 17,254 of 17,254, its cross-check, its 9 decisions), `replication/queues/`, `docs/reviews/desktop-tools-signature-pack-2026-11-16.md` section 24. It has no faults file of its own; the one ruling it left (storage, 552.7 GW in the file's parts against Berkeley Lab's "approximately 749 GW") is cited here and not written again.

## What this reader still cannot get from the tool, and why

- **The queue after the end of 2025.** The file is Berkeley Lab's yearly edition; the next replaces every figure. The operators' own lists, newer, are on the explorer for CAISO, ERCOT and SPP; NYISO's, MISO's and ISO-NE's are held for a license.
- **A wait for a request made today.** Every wait is of requests that were built, by the year they began operating; a recent year's median stands on its fastest requests (row 11).
- **A load's time to connect.** No load is in the file (row 7). ERCOT's large-load report is on `/datacenters`; most utilities publish no measured wait.
- **The storage in hybrids, a request's size or cost, the developer, the upgrades asked for, why a request withdrew.** The parts are in the file and not in the table (row 13); the rest is in no public edition.
- **ERCOT's hybrids' withdrawal rate.** The file labels none withdrawn (rows 3 and 4).
