## How it was read, and what could not be

- **As drawn:** the built HTML and screenshots of session 1857 at 1280 and 390. The 1280 screenshot stops near row 280 of 293, so the charts and the sums table were read from the HTML.
- **The clicks through the page's own functions:** `lib/policymonitor.ts` transpiled without a build and run in Node on `monitor.json` (`runs/s1875/csvtest.mjs`, `sortfig.mjs`): the 293 rows the view opens with, every sort, the calendar on the page's day (2026-11-12), the CSV, the Python and Stata readers. The CSV was read back with the page's own Python reader (pandas: 293 rows, 23 columns, the 27 quoted fields holding "#" read whole). Stata is not on this machine, so the do-file was read, not run. The open row, the hovers and the charts' hover were read from the components' code: no browser was run, since the session may not build or serve the site.
- **Which document each plain read is of:** the live set's `policy_reads` (anon key, read only, 408 rows), matched to the file's 291 plain reads (290 matched).
- **Twenty impact reads against their source text** (largest significance first; EPA 3, NRC 3, DOE 4, FERC 5, BOEM, BLM, PUCT, CPUC, IRS one each; 5 impact sentences and 15 plain reads): the texts held in `tests/fixtures/policy/texts`, `runs/s1857/texts` and `warehouse/raw/policy_reads/recheck_s157/texts`, and five fetched by this session from the Federal Register's API after its robots.txt (2026-15040, 2026-22609, 2026-02246, 2026-22608, 2026-19072; `runs/s1875/texts/`). Nineteen hold to their own document's text, and one states more than its text (row 25); four of the nineteen are drawn under another document's title (rows 12 and 13), which leaves fifteen with no fault (row 24). The Texas Governor's releases were not read: their pages are not in the Federal Register and were not fetched.
- **The policy games and the Digest's policy row** were left untouched, as the spec asks. `/policy` and `?view=week` were checked for width only (390 of 390 at 390 px).
