## Method

1. **The columns read** are the ones whose values name a company (the list in the table above). Nothing is read from free text, except the policy mentions (point 5), which add no company.
2. **Names from news-extracted tables** (`energy_deals`, the datacenter tables) are split where a party lists several ("Nvidia, Amazon and Microsoft"), and a party that is not a company name is left out, by the project's own rule (`warehouse/companies/seed_from_deals.py`, `split_names` and `not_a_company`, the ruling of session 28: descriptions, countries, governments and public bodies, laws, universities). Names from `energy_projects` are registered names (EIA-860M's operator, ERCOT's interconnecting entity) and are not split.
3. **One addition to that rule**, this session's: a name containing "Department of", "Dept of", "Bureau of" or a military word (Air Force, Space Force, Army, Navy, Coast Guard, Corps of Engineers) is a government department and is left out. 19 names, 18 of them EIA-860M operators and one deal buyer, "Department of Energy" (which `energy_companies` holds as `erwco:department-of-energy`, the one row of that table the seed leaves out). The Los Angeles Department of Water & Power, a municipal utility, falls under it; utilities named "City of" (384 rows), public power districts, authorities and cooperatives are kept. 243 names are left out in all: 96 descriptions, 91 universities (90 of them EIA-860M operators of campus plants), 24 countries, 31 government bodies, 1 law. Each is in `runs/chain_oct21_third/s443/left_out.csv` with its reason and column.
4. **Merge rule: the project's own.** Two spellings are one company only when `name_key` (in `warehouse/thesis/build.py`: lower case, no parentheses, punctuation or legal suffix such as Inc, LLC, Corp) gives the same key; the name shown is the most frequent spelling, and the others are in `other_spellings` (152 rows have one). Nothing else is merged. "Amazon" and "Amazon Web Services" are two rows, as are "Ormat" (one deal) and "Ormat Nevada Inc" (64 project rows): a name that may be a parent, a subsidiary or a project company cannot be shown to be the same company from the tables alone.
5. **Policy mentions.** The policy tables' `parties` are agencies (FERC, NRC, the Energy Department and others); no column names a company. A company of the seed gains a mention where its full name stands, case-sensitive and as whole words, in source text: `policy_actions.title` and `.abstract`, `policy_reads.title`, `policy_reads_evidence.span` (all text the publisher wrote; `plain_read` and `why`, written by a model, are not read). A one-word name shorter than six letters is not searched, and a match whose next or previous word is capitalized does not count ("Outer Continental Shelf" is not Continental; "Duke Energy Carolinas" is not Duke Energy), nor does a name inside a list of capitalized places. The mentions are in their own column, `policy_mentions`, apart from the columns that name a company, because a text match is a lead to check, not a fact: one counted match on 21 October, "Liberty has 30 days ...", may not be the deal party "Liberty".
6. **Fields, and where each comes from.** `description` and `stage` only from an `energy_companies` row the Thesis Builder researched (its `sources` column holds the public pages each field was checked against); "not disclosed" is not a stage. `segment` only from `energy_companies.sector`, and `segment_source` says which kind: for a deal party it is the news scorer's sector of the stories of its deals (`seed_from_deals.py`), not a segment of the company itself; for a Thesis Builder row it is "energy technology". **The master plan defines no segment taxonomy**: tool 10 names "what each does, stage, funding, thesis fit" and tool 11 "by segment, stage, and investor", without a list of segments. `documents` names the Thesis Builder workbooks in `docs/thesis/` (sheets Landscape and Incumbents) that list the company (20 rows). Every row's `tables` gives each table and how many of its rows name the company, `roles` the table and column, and `row_ids` up to 10 row ids a table with the count of the rest; `runs/chain_oct21_third/s443/company_rows.csv` holds every reference uncapped.
7. **Two names of the thesis's list are not rows**: "Nabors Industries" and "Ormat Technologies" stand in the Incumbents sheets of the geothermal workbooks and in no ERW table (the tables have "Ormat" and Ormat's project companies, not merged, point 4).

## What the data machine needs to load it as a table

- **Shape.** `entities` (`docs/datastandard.md`, shape (b)), `entity_type` `company`, one row a company. The required columns map as: `entity_id` from `name_key`, in the namespace `energy_companies` already uses (`erwco:<slug>`), so that the 468 rows that match keep their ids; `name` as the seed's; `source` `erw:companies_seed` (a new `sources.csv` row) or, better, the seed folded into `energy_companies` itself as a third origin beside `erw:energy_deals` and `erw:thesis_builder`. The references go to a second table in the `events` shape or a plain link table (`company_id`, `table`, `row_id`, `column`), not into one cell: Google alone is named by 143 rows.
- **Key.** `entity_id`, unique; `name_key` unique within the table (the seed has 0 duplicates of either, `tests/test_session443.py`). A merge across spellings that `name_key` does not join (a parent and its project companies) needs a ruling and a rule, not a guess.
- **License.** `energy_companies` is public (`docs/licenses.md`: "the ERW's own work"). The seed's names come from tables held since 21 October: `energy_deals` and the datacenter tables need written permission from news publishers, `energy_projects` from ISO-NE (`docs/licenses.md`, the holds). A name and a row id are facts, not the publishers' text, but the loader should give each row a license from the strictest publisher behind it, the split-table method of session 441 (`docs/reviews/sources-by-row-2026-10-21.md`): 6,457 companies rest only on EIA-860M (EIA, public), 1,123 only on ERCOT's queue, 164 on both; 509 rest on at least one news-extracted table. The 19 government departments and 91 universities left out stay out until Samuel rules otherwise.
- **The run.** A step in the daily run after `energy_projects` and `seed_from_deals.py`, under the data lock, validated by `erw_validate.py` like any table. The seed is a snapshot of 21 October and goes stale with each run.
