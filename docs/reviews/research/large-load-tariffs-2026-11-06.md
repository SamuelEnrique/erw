# Large-load tariffs and connection rules: a sourced research pass of 6 November 2026

Energy Research Warehouse (ERW). Written by Claude in chat with the owner on 6 November 2026, from three parallel research passes over commission dockets, filed tariff books and operator documents (retrieval date 6 November 2026 throughout). It exists because the data machine's chains 147 to 157 could not read most of these publishers (reCAPTCHA at PUCO, Virginia's SCC paused, North Carolina and Nevada name the reader, AEP's terms, Duke's 403), and the large-load dataset is what the owner told a16z the data machine builds.

## How the machines use this file

- Every figure here carries a document URL, a page or sheet, and a quote. **No figure enters `utility_large_load_tariffs` or `large_load_connection_rules` from this file alone.** A session verifies each row against its cited document (the document fetched, robots and terms of its host read first; a host this file lists as refusing is not asked again by the reader, the document is taken from a mirror the file names or the row waits), then writes the row with the citation. A figure marked "secondhand" or "not verified against primary" is not written; it goes to the row's `notes` as a lead.
- The owner's ruling of 6 November (docs/state/rulings_2026-11-06.md, item 4): a row sourced to a commission docket or a filed tariff book is `public`; a row whose only source is a host whose terms ask written permission is `internal` until the permission is on record.
- The three sets below are the researchers' tables as delivered, each followed by its sources and gaps. Grid assignments in the tables are the researchers' characterization, not quoted from the documents.
- Downloaded copies of the primary documents sit in the chat session's scratch folder (not in git); each row's URL is the citation.

## What the three sets found, in one breath

- **Adopted tariffs with full rate sheets read:** AEP Ohio Schedule DCT (85 percent minimum, 50/65/80/90 ramp, 12-year term, collateral 50 percent of minimum charges); Evergy Missouri Metro and West LLPS (75 MW, 80 percent, 12 plus 5 years, two years' minimum bills as collateral, demand charges stated); Ameren Missouri LLCS (75 MW at 115 kV or above, 80 percent, 12 plus 5, summer demand 22.43 USD per kW); Dominion GS-4 (85 and 60 percent floors from 2027); Entergy Louisiana LLHLFPS-L (70 MW, blocks) and LPHLF-G (200 MW); Georgia Power TOU-SC-15 with the 100 MW rules; SRP E-67 (20 MW, 80 percent of forecast); APS XHLF; Alabama Power LPL; Oncor and CenterPoint transmission service with Rider TCRF on 4CP (no large-load schedule yet); PGE's Order 26-154 (20 MW, 90 percent, 10 to 30 years).
- **Ordered but not yet in a sheet:** Dominion GS-5 (effective 1 January 2027, 14 years, 1.5 million USD per MW collateral); Duke Energy Carolinas and Progress Large Load Tariff (settlement of 6 October 2026, 75 percent, 10 or 15 years, pending NCUC approval).
- **Pending proposals:** Xcel Colorado Schedule TL (50 MW, 80 percent, 15 years); APS's revised XHLF; NV Energy's LLESAs; PUCT Project 58000 (20-year agreements); Evergy Kansas LLPS (approved, sheet unread).
- **Connection rules:** ERCOT PGRR 115 and the LLIS with its business-day clocks and 14,000 USD fee; PUCT 16 TAC 25.194 (75 MW, 100,000 USD study fee, 50,000 USD per MW security; adoption order text not retrieved); PJM's 50 MW definition, the EIT (500,000 USD deposit) and the Show Cause proposal's 12-month cycle; MISO ERAS (3 months to a GIA); SPP HILL (10 MW at 69 kV or below, 50 MW above); CAISO's straw proposal (50 MW, PTO studies in 60 to 90 days); FERC's six show-cause orders of 18 June 2026.
