Rules: the fact is always an energy fact first (a number or statistic from the warehouse, a piece of energy history, a quirk of how the system works); the second half is optional and rotates among structures (a comparison of scale, an on-this-day history line, an etymology or cross-field connection, a light pun); a cross-field angle is used some days and never by default. A digest without a fun fact ships rather than one with an unverified fact. Seed the bank with 20 verified facts now and add one per run. Add the section to the email.
